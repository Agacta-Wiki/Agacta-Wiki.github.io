# Currency of Agacta

Agacta's national currency is the **Valuto**, written with the symbol **Ʋ** and the currency code **AGV**.

**1 Valuto = 100 Centi Valuti**

At the current reference exchange rate:

> **€1.00 = Ʋ3.62**

Approximate equivalents:

| Ʋ1 | €0.276 |

## Denominations

### Coins

The current coin series consists of:

- 10 centi
- 20 centi
- 50 centi
- Ʋ1
- Ʋ2
- Ʋ5

The former **1 centi**, **2 centi**, and **5 centi** coins have been discontinued.

The size of Agactan coins increases with denomination, with the highest-value coins physically larger than lower-value pieces.

| Denomination | Material | Animal |
|---|---|---|
| 10 centi | Bronze | Octopus |
| 20 centi | Silver | Grey horse |
| 50 centi | Gold | Blue whale |
| Ʋ1 | Silver | Heron |
| Ʋ2 | Gold | Agactan lynx |
| Ʋ5 | Silver and gold, bimetallic | Agactan hare |

The **Ʋ5 coin** was introduced in **2009**.

### Banknotes

Banknotes are issued in the following denominations:

- Ʋ10
- Ʋ20
- Ʋ50
- Ʋ100
- Ʋ200
- Ʋ500

## Animal symbolism

Animals play an important role in Agactan national symbolism and are prominently represented on the country's coinage.

### Heron

The **heron** is the national bird of Agacta and one of the country's most recognisable national symbols. Its cultural position is comparable to that of the cockerel in France.

The heron appears on the **Ʋ1 coin**.

### Agactan lynx

The **Agactan lynx** is the national animal of Agacta and another major symbol of the country.

Historically, the lynx was associated with the **House of Agacta** during the kingdom period. Following the establishment of the republic, the animal's symbolism broadened beyond the former royal house and it became a national emblem of the republic itself.

The Agactan lynx appears on the **Ʋ2 coin**.

### Blue whale

The **blue whale** is Agacta's national aquatic mammal. Blue whales are deeply admired in Agactan culture and are particularly abundant in the waters around **Altur**.

The blue whale appears on the **50 centi coin**.

### Grey horse

The **grey horse** is a traditional Agactan symbol, particularly associated with the country's prairie regions.

It appears on the **20 centi coin**.

### Octopus

The **octopus** is traditionally associated with wisdom and intelligence in Agactan culture.

Unlike in many coastal cultures, octopus is **not considered a food animal in Agacta** and is not traditionally eaten.

It appears on the **10 centi coin**.

### Agactan hare

The **Agactan hare** appears on the **Ʋ5 coin**. It was selected as the design for the denomination when the coin was introduced in **2009**.

## Coin design

The modern Agactan coin series uses differences in size, colour, and metal composition to make denominations easy to distinguish.

Lower denominations use single-colour metallic finishes, while the Ʋ5 coin uses a distinctive **silver-and-gold bimetallic design**. Coin size increases progressively with value.

Together, the animal designs form a miniature gallery of Agactan natural and cultural symbolism, ranging from marine life and prairie traditions to symbols closely associated with the state itself.
