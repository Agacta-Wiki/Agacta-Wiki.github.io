# Siva Avarka Economic Zone

The **Siva Avarka Economic Zone (SAEZ)** is an economic framework connecting ten Alturian nations. It focuses on **free trade and the movement of people**, while preserving the political independence and distinct identities of its members.

## Purpose and scope

SAEZ facilitates free trade and the movement of people between participating countries. Its purpose is economic cooperation rather than political integration.

The zone has **no common parliament, flag or political identity**. It does not establish a common foreign policy or defence policy. Member states retain their own national identities and independent positions in international affairs.

## Countries outside SAEZ

Several countries remain outside the zone for different political, geographical or sovereignty-related reasons.

| Country | Established position |
| --- | --- |
| **Alaram** | Outside SAEZ; aligned with Ricko. |
| **Sago** | Outside SAEZ; aligned with Ricko. |
| **Avarka Major** | Outside SAEZ; neutral. |
| **Grastin** | Outside SAEZ; neutral. |
| **Flyma** | Outside SAEZ because of concerns over rules and national autonomy. |
| **Burton** | Outside SAEZ because of distance and its emphasis on sovereignty over border control. |

These positions do not imply that non-members have identical relationships with SAEZ countries. Economic cooperation, diplomatic relations and strategic alignment remain distinct considerations.

## Relationship with SADS

SAEZ is separate from the **Siva–Avarka Defense Strategy (SADS)**.

| Organisation | Established purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **SAEZ** | Free trade and movement of people |
| **SADS** | Military cooperation |

Membership of one organisation does not automatically entail membership of the other.

**Flyma** belongs to SADS but remains outside SAEZ. Conversely, **Foristia, Sypthburg, Larshoburg and Icovold** belong to SAEZ but are not on the established SADS membership list.

**Burton** belongs to neither organisation, despite its close strategic alignment with SADS and its cooperation with members on a case-by-case basis.
