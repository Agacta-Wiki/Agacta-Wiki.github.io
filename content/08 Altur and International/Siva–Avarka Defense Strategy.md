# Siva–Avarka Defense Strategy

The **Siva–Avarka Defense Strategy (SADS)** is a framework for military cooperation between eight Alturian nations: **Agacta, Sensin, Luum, Flyma, Skiburg, Sudo, Singa and Slica**.

Members retain their own foreign policies and distinct international relationships. Participation in SADS does not require every member to adopt the same diplomatic position towards countries outside the organisation.

## Nature and purpose

SADS provides a framework for military cooperation. It is not a political union, and its members remain independent states with their own foreign-policy priorities.

Different approaches coexist within the organisation. Some members place particular emphasis on diplomacy and maintaining constructive relations with countries outside SADS.

## Origins

SADS developed from existing international relationships and security concerns. Agacta's longstanding relationship with **Luum** predates the organisation.

The formation of SADS therefore belongs to a later stage in the development of those relationships; it was not the origin of Agactan–Luumian cooperation.

## Agacta and Sudo

**Agacta and Sudo** are among SADS's most diplomatic and relatively neutral members. Their approaches emphasise diplomacy, peacekeeping and medical missions.

Both maintain relatively cordial relations with **Alaram and Ricko**, compared with some other members of SADS. Their participation in the organisation coexists with a willingness to maintain diplomatic channels beyond it.

In this context, their relative neutrality describes their approach within SADS rather than non-membership.

## Relations with non-members

### Burton

**Burton** is not a member of SADS but is closely aligned with it strategically. It maintains a strong army, sovereign control over its borders and a high degree of strategic autonomy.

Burton cooperates on a case-by-case basis rather than through formal membership. It is also outside SAEZ.

### Ricko

**Ricko** is outside SADS and has questioned the organisation's continued existence. Relations with individual SADS members vary, with Agacta and Sudo maintaining relatively cordial ties.

### Alaram

**Alaram** is outside SADS and is broadly aligned with Ricko. Its position does not prevent it from maintaining relations with individual SADS members, including Agacta.

### Avarka Major

**Avarka Major** is neutral and is not a member of SADS. Cooperation with Agacta and other member states does not in itself imply membership or military alignment.

## Relationship with SAEZ

SADS is distinct from **SAEZ**, the economic framework for free trade and free movement.

| Organisation | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **SADS** | Military cooperation |
| **SAEZ** | Economic cooperation through free trade and free movement |

SAEZ is not a political union and has no common parliament, flag, political identity, foreign policy or defence policy.

The two organisations have different memberships. **Flyma**, for example, belongs to SADS but is outside SAEZ. Membership of one organisation does not automatically imply membership of the other.

## Cooperation beyond SADS

SADS membership is distinct from other forms of cooperation between Alturian nations. Countries can work with Agacta and other members without belonging to the organisation.

The use of Agacta's **Aquatrip launch centre** and participation in multinational space expeditions do not, by themselves, indicate SADS membership. These activities have not been established as SADS programmes.
