# Vunter System

The **Vunter system** is the planetary system containing **Altur**. It has **ten planets**, numbered in order of their distance from the star. Altur is the fourth planet.

## Planetary designations

Most Alturian languages have their own names for the planets. In international space operations, the planets are identified using **Vunter** followed by their orbital number, from **Vunter 1** to **Vunter 10**.

Altur is therefore designated **Vunter 4** in this shared terminology.

## Habitable zone

**Vunter 3, Vunter 4 and Vunter 5** lie within the system's habitable zone.

Their position within this zone does not imply identical environments. The surface conditions, presence of life and suitability for settlement on Vunter 3 and Vunter 5 have not yet been established.

## Space exploration

The Vunter system is the focus of the operational space programmes of Altur's nations, including the **Agacta Space Organisation (ASO)**.

Typical Agactan scientific expeditions last approximately **six months to two years**. Specialist expeditions to the system's distant outer reaches involve deployments of approximately **three to four years** and are usually multinational.
