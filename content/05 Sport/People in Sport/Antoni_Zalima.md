# Antoni Zalima

**Antoni Zalima** is an Agactan soccer manager and former player. He is the manager of [[SK Aquatrip]] and is closely identified with the club after spending almost his entire playing career there.

## Playing career

Zalima was as loyal to Aquatrip as almost any player of his generation. He spent the great majority of his career with [[SK Aquatrip]], leaving only for three brief spells.

He played one season for [[Riala Komtio SK]] and another for [[Tlelmund Sport]] during a period in which Aquatrip were outside the top flight. He also spent one season on loan with [[Merino Jimnastika]]. Apart from those exceptions, his playing career belonged entirely to Aquatrip.

## Managerial career

### SK Mlentrokerk

Zalima began his senior managerial career with [[SK Mlentrokerk]], one of the clubs within the wider Aquatrip franchise. He managed Mlentrokerk during the 2019–20 and 2020–21 seasons.

### SK Aquatrip

Zalima was appointed manager of [[SK Aquatrip]] ahead of the 2021–22 season. His first year was a difficult relegation battle, but Aquatrip survived in eleventh place.

The club then climbed to seventh in 2022–23. Two eighth-place finishes, in 2023–24 and 2025–26, came either side of another seventh-place campaign in 2024–25.

Although Aquatrip have not broken into the championship race under Zalima, his tenure has been a successful period of stability. After the danger of his first season, the club established itself securely in the middle of Division 1.

## Reputation

Zalima's identity is inseparable from Aquatrip. As both a long-serving player and a steady manager, he represents continuity at a club that has previously experienced relegation and instability. His five completed seasons in charge have produced survival followed by four consecutive mid-table finishes.
