# Lodovik Gala

**Lodovik Gala** is an Agactan soccer manager and former player. He is the manager of [[Montanomund KSK]], whom he guided to sixth place in the 2025–26 Agactan Division 1 season.

## Playing career

Gala was developed as a player by [[Nleto Distraktiva Klub]]. His playing career did not achieve the same prominence as his later work in management, but his education at Nleto provided the foundation for his coaching career.

## Managerial career

### Hojomund Distraktiva Klub

Gala began his managerial career with [[Hojomund Distraktiva Klub]]. He spent two successful seasons with the club, culminating in a second-place finish in Division 2.

His work at Hojomund attracted the attention of newly promoted [[Montanomund KSK]], who appointed him ahead of their return to the top flight.

### Montanomund KSK

Gala's first task at Montanomund was survival. The club finished eleventh in 2024–25, narrowly achieving that objective.

Montanomund then made a dramatic improvement in 2025–26. Gala led them to a surprising sixth-place finish, transforming a side expected to face another relegation battle into one of the season's strongest overachievers.

## Reputation

Gala is regarded as one of the most promising younger managers in Agactan soccer. His Montanomund spell has shown two different strengths: first securing survival under pressure and then building a side capable of finishing in the top half.
