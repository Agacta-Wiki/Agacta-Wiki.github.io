# Abelo Vintra

**Abelo Vintra** is an Agactan soccer manager and former player from Bantro. He is the manager of [[Merino Jimnastika]], having returned to the club for a second spell in 2025–26.

Vintra is a three-time Agactan championship-winning manager, having won the title with Merino in 2011–12 and with [[Nleto Distraktiva Klub]] in both 2019–20 and 2020–21.

## Playing career

Vintra began his playing career in Bantro. He subsequently represented [[Setroburg Olimpik]], [[Nleto Distraktiva Klub]] and [[Montanomund KSK]].

## Managerial career

### Montanomund KSK

Vintra led [[Montanomund KSK]] to promotion in 2006–07. He then established the club in Division 1 with eighth-place finishes in both 2007–08 and 2008–09.

### First spell at Merino Jimnastika

Vintra joined [[Merino Jimnastika]] in 2010. The defining achievement of his first spell was the 2011–12 championship, won during the celebrated season in which Merino and [[Orientoport RK]] disrupted the traditional title hierarchy.

Merino remained competitive after the title, finishing third in both 2012–13 and 2013–14. A fifth-place finish followed in 2014–15, after which Vintra and the club parted by mutual agreement.

### Astroburg

After leaving Merino, Vintra led Astroburg to promotion. His subsequent top-flight spell ended in dismissal with the club nine points from safety.

### Nleto Distraktiva Klub

Vintra rebuilt his reputation spectacularly at [[Nleto Distraktiva Klub]]. Nleto won consecutive championships in 2019–20 and 2020–21 before finishing third in 2021–22.

He was dismissed in January 2023 with Nleto ninth in the table, bringing an abrupt end to a period that had delivered two league titles.

### Aulburg

Vintra next managed Aulburg. The club finished eleventh in 2023–24 and fifteenth in 2024–25, after which he was dismissed.

### Return to Merino Jimnastika

Merino brought Vintra back for the 2025–26 season in a move widely presented as the “Return of the King.” He immediately restored the club to the upper end of the table, guiding them to fourth place.

## Reputation

Vintra's career combines long-term success with sharp changes in fortune. His three championships place him among the outstanding managers of the modern era, while the difficult endings at Nleto, Astroburg and Aulburg help explain why his return to Merino carried such emotional and professional significance.
