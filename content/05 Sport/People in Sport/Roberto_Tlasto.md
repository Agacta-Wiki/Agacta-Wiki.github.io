# Roberto Tlasto

**Roberto Tlasto** is an Agactan soccer manager and former defensive midfielder. He is the manager of [[Kalisto Balklub]], whom he guided to consecutive second-place finishes in 2022–23 and 2023–24.

Tlasto is most closely associated with [[Setroburg Olimpik]], where he was a talismanic player and later briefly held what he regarded as his dream managerial job. His coaching career has also included successful spells with [[Nordlako Olimpik]] and [[SK Aquatrip]].

## Playing career

Tlasto was a talismanic defensive midfielder who spent the predominant part of his playing career with [[Setroburg Olimpik]]. His influence came through positional intelligence, leadership and his ability to provide the structure around which more attacking players could operate.

He retired at the end of the 2013–14 season. His long association with Setroburg made managing the senior side his principal ambition after moving into coaching.

## Managerial career

### Nordlako Olimpik

Tlasto began his managerial career with [[Nordlako Olimpik]], one of the clubs within the wider Setroburg franchise. His impressive work there during the seasons following his retirement quickly established him as a promising coach.

Although he was strongly identified with Setroburg, his first Division 1 opportunity came elsewhere when [[SK Aquatrip]] appointed him ahead of the 2016–17 season.

### SK Aquatrip

Tlasto made an immediate impact at Aquatrip. He led the club to fourth place in 2016–17 and repeated that finish in 2017–18.

The consecutive fourth-place finishes represented a major achievement for a club operating outside Agacta's traditional championship powers. They demonstrated Tlasto's ability to organise a competitive side and maximise the talent available to him.

His work at Aquatrip attracted the attention of Setroburg. When the senior position became available in 2018, Tlasto was offered the opportunity to return to the club with which he was most closely associated.

### Setroburg Olimpik

Tlasto described the Setroburg position as his dream job. His first season appeared to justify the appointment, as Setroburg finished second in 2018–19.

The following campaign was much less successful. Setroburg fell to fifth in 2019–20, a poor return by the standards of one of Agacta's Big Three. Despite his status as a former club talisman, Tlasto was relieved of his duties after only two seasons.

The dismissal represented the first major setback of his managerial career. He remained out of management throughout the 2020–21 season.

### Kalisto Balklub

[[Kalisto Balklub]] offered Tlasto a route back into management in summer 2021. The appointment paired a historically significant club seeking its first championship since 1990–91 with a coach whose work at Aquatrip and first season at Setroburg suggested that he could build a title challenger.

Kalisto finished fourth in Tlasto's first season. They then mounted consecutive championship challenges, finishing second in both 2022–23 and 2023–24. Those results represented Kalisto's strongest sustained attempt in decades to end their long wait for a sixth league title.

The club slipped to fourth in 2024–25 before a troubled 2025–26 campaign ended with a seventh-place finish. It was Kalisto's first bottom-half finish under Tlasto and placed renewed pressure on the manager entering 2026–27.

Tlasto retains considerable credit for the two runner-up finishes, but Kalisto now need to demonstrate that the project has not run its course. A convincing return to the upper end of the table is expected in 2026–27.

## Style and reputation

Tlasto's background as a defensive midfielder is reflected in his emphasis on structure, pressing discipline and control of central areas. He is not an inherently defensive manager: his Kalisto teams can commit numerous creative players to attack, but their freedom depends upon carefully defined responsibilities behind them.

He has shown a willingness to use unconventional formations to exploit the strengths of his squad. At Kalisto, that tactical flexibility has included systems built around a lone holding midfielder, mobile attacking midfielders and forwards capable of dropping away from the conventional striker position.

Tlasto's reputation rests largely upon three achievements: consecutive fourth-place finishes with Aquatrip, taking Setroburg to second in his first season and leading Kalisto to consecutive runner-up finishes. The principal criticism is that his teams have not yet converted a championship challenge into a league title.

The 2026–27 season is therefore an important test. Tlasto must show that the decline from second to fourth and then seventh was temporary rather than evidence that his Kalisto cycle is nearing its conclusion.
