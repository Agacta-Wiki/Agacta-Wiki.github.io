# Alvio Navelo

**Alvio Navelo** is an Agactan soccer manager and former player from Treslio. He is the manager of [[Tlelmund Sport]] and is widely regarded as a veteran relegation-survival specialist.

Navelo's long career has been defined by defensive soccer, rescue assignments and frequent clashes with boards and supporters. Although often compared to Mike Bassett, his record includes a remarkable rise with [[Tleve Atleto]] and numerous successful escapes from relegation.

## Playing career

Navelo had a relatively unremarkable playing career with [[Sporting Zogyo]] and [[Tleve Atleto]]. His reputation was made almost entirely through management.

## Managerial career

### Oktave Atleto and Tleve Atleto

Navelo began his managerial career with [[Oktave Atleto]], Tleve's developmental team, in 1999–00. He remained in charge in 2000–01 and for the first half of 2001–02.

His promotion to the senior [[Tleve Atleto]] job came in extraordinary circumstances. Oktave defeated their own parent club in a League Cup tie while Tleve sat last in Division 2 and faced the genuine possibility of being relegated into the same division as their developmental side. Tleve responded by appointing the manager who had just beaten them.

Navelo steadied the club and guided them to sixth in 2001–02. Tleve subsequently finished third in 2002–03, seventh in 2003–04 and fourth in 2004–05 before winning Division 2 in 2005–06.

Despite operating on a fraction of the budget available to most top-flight clubs, Tleve finished tenth in both 2006–07 and 2007–08. Navelo could not repeat the escape in 2008–09. He was dismissed in April as Tleve desperately attempted to avoid relegation, which was ultimately confirmed.

### Orientoport RK

[[Orientoport RK]] surprised Agacta by appointing Navelo ahead of the 2010–11 season. His much more defensive approach immediately ruffled feathers at a club accustomed to expansive soccer.

The appointment was a failure. Navelo was dismissed in November with a team expected to challenge for ACFA qualification languishing in tenth place.

### Nlastromund RRK

At Christmas 2010, [[Nlastromund RRK]] appointed Navelo with the club stranded at the bottom of Division 1. He kept them up through a victory on the final day of the 2010–11 season.

Navelo remained for the beginning of 2011–12 but was dismissed after a poor start. Nlastromund eventually finished tenth under his successor.

### SK Aquatrip

Navelo took charge of [[SK Aquatrip]] in March 2012. On this occasion the rescue attempt failed: Aquatrip finished twelfth and were relegated, and Navelo was dismissed.

### Sporting Zogyo and Kalisto Balklub

He rebuilt his reputation with [[Sporting Zogyo]] in 2012–13. Zogyo were second in Division 2 in December when [[Kalisto Balklub]], newly promoted but rooted to the bottom of Division 1, approached him.

Navelo again produced a successful escape, guiding Kalisto to eleventh and preventing an immediate return to Division 2. He was dismissed in January 2014 following a poor run of form and clashes with the club's board.

### Inter Stototrip

Navelo joined [[Inter Stototrip]] midway through 2014–15, when the club had unexpectedly become involved in a relegation battle. He kept Stototrip in Division 1 through intensely defensive soccer.

Despite achieving survival, he was released following arguments with the board. He had also failed to win over the club's supporters.

### OSK Abeldrip

In January 2017, Navelo took over [[OSK Abeldrip]] with the club at the bottom of the table. He led them to tenth and survival in 2016–17, followed by eighth in 2017–18 and another tenth-place survival in 2018–19.

The 2019–20 campaign was much less successful. Abeldrip were last when the league was suspended in March. Navelo's contract expired on 30 June and was not renewed, one day before the competition was due to resume. Abeldrip ultimately finished twelfth and were relegated.

### Riala Komtio SK

Navelo took charge of [[Riala Komtio SK]] for 2020–21 and guided them to seventh. They improved to sixth in 2021–22 but were relegated after finishing twelfth in 2022–23.

He remained with Riala in Division 2 for 2023–24. After losing the promotion race to [[Montanomund KSK]], Navelo resigned.

### Tlelmund Sport

[[Tlelmund Sport]] appointed Navelo midway through 2024–25 with the club bottom of Division 1. He delivered another successful rescue, taking Tlelmund to tenth.

Tlelmund again survived under Navelo in 2025–26, finishing eleventh and extending his reputation as Agactan soccer's foremost survival specialist.

## Style and reputation

Navelo's teams are organised primarily around defensive security, compact shape and survival rather than aesthetic ambition. That approach has repeatedly made him valuable to clubs in immediate danger, but it has also caused friction at institutions such as Orientoport and Stototrip, where supporters expected more adventurous soccer.

His career contains failures—including relegation with Aquatrip and Riala—but the number and variety of his rescue jobs have made him one of the most recognisable managerial figures in modern Agactan soccer.
