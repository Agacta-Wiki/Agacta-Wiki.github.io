# Josio Leldro

**Josio Leldro** is an Agactan soccer manager and former player from Treslio. He is the manager of [[Inter Stototrip]], whom he led to the Division 2 championship and promotion in 2025–26.

## Playing career

Leldro began his playing career with [[Yojo Sporting]]. After a loan spell with [[Ulos RK]], he returned to Yojo for a further season before graduating to the senior [[Sporting Treslio]] side.

Following several years with Sporting, he moved abroad to Hihvall in Sensin, where he played from 2004 until 2009. He then returned to Sporting Treslio for the final two seasons of his playing career.

## Managerial career

### Yojo Sporting

Leldro began his managerial career with [[Yojo Sporting]] and spent two years in charge. He eventually left because he believed he was unlikely to be offered the senior Sporting Treslio position.

### Mlaxa AK

Leldro took charge of [[Mlaxa AK]] for the 2018–19 season and immediately won promotion back to Division 2. Mlaxa finished a comfortable sixth in 2019–20 before securing another promotion in 2020–21, this time returning to Division 1.

The club finished ninth in 2021–22, comfortably above the relegation places. Survival was much more dramatic in 2022–23, when Mlaxa escaped on the final day and finished eleventh.

Leldro was dismissed in February 2024 with Mlaxa bottom of Division 1. A cup defeat to lower-ranked [[Menha Jimnastika]] contributed to the decision.

### Inter Stototrip

[[Inter Stototrip]] appointed Leldro in June 2024. His first season produced a third-place finish in Division 2. In 2025–26, Stototrip won the division and earned promotion to the top flight.

## Reputation

Leldro has developed a reputation as a promotion specialist. He has twice rebuilt clubs outside the top flight, first taking Mlaxa through two divisions and later returning Inter Stototrip to Division 1 after a two-season project.
