# Avrius Hlemarb

**Avrius Hlemarb** is a Sago-born soccer manager and former player. He is the manager of [[Nleto Distraktiva Klub]]. His appointment marked his first playing or managerial involvement in Agactan soccer.

## Playing career

Hlemarb began his playing career in Sago before moving abroad. He later represented Flymese Losach and the historically prestigious Ricko's Runga, before finishing his career in Flyma with Lu Vascrin.

He neither played nor managed in Agacta before accepting the Nleto position, making him an unusually external appointment for one of the country's leading clubs.

## Managerial career

### Fastok

Hlemarb made his managerial reputation with Fastok, the dominant club in Sago. He led the club to three consecutive national championships, establishing himself as one of the most successful managers working in a league regarded as broadly comparable in standard to Agacta's.

### Lu Ville

He subsequently moved to Flyma to manage Lu Ville. That appointment was considerably less successful. Hlemarb left the club in February with Lu Ville twelfth in a 20-team league and their prospects of ACFA qualification effectively gone.

### Nleto Distraktiva Klub

[[Nleto Distraktiva Klub]] appointed Hlemarb after dismissing [[Jakubo Lentro]] following a disappointing ACFA campaign. The decision reflected Nleto's belief that his championship record in Sago could translate to Agacta.

His arrival nevertheless represented a calculated risk. Hlemarb came with a strong record of winning domestic titles but no direct experience of Agactan players, clubs or soccer culture.

## Reputation

Hlemarb arrived in Agacta with two sharply contrasting parts to his record: sustained dominance with Fastok and a disappointing spell at Lu Ville. At Nleto, the central question is whether his success in Sago offers a better guide to his abilities than his difficult experience in Flyma.
