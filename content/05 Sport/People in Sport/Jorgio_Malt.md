# Jorgio Malt

**Jorgio Malt** is an Agactan soccer manager and former player. He is the manager of [[Riala Komtio SK]], whom he led to the Division 2 championship and promotion in 2024–25.

Malt followed a traditional route through the ASC system. After spending most of his playing career with Riala, he entered management with developmental club [[Sentrotrip]] before being promoted to the senior position following Riala's relegation in 2022–23.

## Playing career

Malt spent most of his playing career with [[Riala Komtio SK]] and became closely identified with the club. His time away from Riala consisted principally of two-year spells with [[Merino Jimnastika]] and [[Kalisto Balklub]].

Those experiences allowed him to work within two other major ASC organisations before eventually returning to the Riala structure. His playing position has not yet been established.

## Managerial career

### Sentrotrip

Malt began coaching [[Sentrotrip]], one of the clubs within the Riala franchise, in 2019–20. His appointment represented the conventional ASC pathway for a former senior player: learning management at developmental level before being considered for the principal provincial team.

He remained with Sentrotrip through the 2022–23 season. When Riala finished twelfth in Division 1 and were relegated, the franchise promoted Malt to take charge of the senior side.

### Riala Komtio SK

Malt's first season as Riala manager produced a credible third-place finish in Division 2. Although the club missed out on promotion in 2023–24, the board retained faith in its internally developed coach.

That patience was emphatically rewarded in 2024–25. Riala dominated Division 2, secured promotion with five matches remaining and returned to the top flight after a two-season absence.

The immediate objective for 2025–26 was survival. Malt achieved it without requiring a final-day escape, guiding Riala to a respectable ninth-place finish in their first season back in Division 1.

The result strengthened his position and completed a clear early progression: developmental coaching, promotion to the senior team, an unsuccessful first challenge, a dominant championship season and successful top-flight consolidation.

## Reputation

Malt is regarded as a product of the Riala system rather than an external specialist recruited for a short-term rescue. He understands the franchise from both its developmental and senior levels and was trusted to continue after failing to win promotion at the first attempt.

The dominant 2024–25 campaign vindicated that decision. Following it with ninth place in Division 1 demonstrated that Riala's success was not confined to the second tier.

His next challenge is to establish the club securely in mid-table. Having delivered promotion and survival, Malt must now show that Riala can progress without losing the identity and continuity that made his appointment attractive.
