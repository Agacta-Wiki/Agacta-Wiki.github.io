# Walter Zemdro

**Walter Zemdro** is an Agactan soccer manager, former player and sports-science graduate. He is the manager of [[Sporting Treslio]], whom he led to consecutive Division 1 championships in 2023–24 and 2024–25.

## Playing and academic career

Zemdro was regarded as a prospect within the [[Merino Jimnastika]] system but spent several seasons playing for [[Oyatrip Jimnastika]]. He later left the Merino organisation and continued his career with [[Ulos RK]].

He retired from playing at the age of 30 and completed a degree in sports science. Zemdro subsequently developed a reputation as an academic and methodical coach, with his formal education becoming an important part of his managerial identity.

## Managerial career

### Menha Jimnastika and Oyatrip Jimnastika

Zemdro's early coaching career included spells with [[Menha Jimnastika]] and his former club [[Oyatrip Jimnastika]]. Those positions allowed him to build experience within the wider Merino structure before receiving the senior job.

### Merino Jimnastika

[[Merino Jimnastika]] appointed Zemdro to lead its senior side in 2018. The club finished fifth in 2018–19, third in 2019–20 and second in 2020–21, producing a steady progression towards the championship.

Merino completed that rise in 2021–22, winning its first Division 1 title in a decade. Zemdro left immediately afterwards to take charge of wealthy rivals [[Sporting Treslio]].

His departure directly after the championship badly damaged his relationship with Merino supporters. He is routinely booed on his returns to the club and is frequently labelled a “traitor” by the home crowd.

### Sporting Treslio

Zemdro's first season at Sporting was a relative disappointment. The club finished third in 2022–23 as [[Setroburg Olimpik]] won the championship.

Sporting responded by winning consecutive titles in 2023–24 and 2024–25. Their ACFA performances under Zemdro have generally been considered satisfactory, although they have not transformed the club's continental standing.

Setroburg reclaimed the title in 2025–26, with Sporting finishing second. That result renewed pressure on Zemdro despite the two championships he had delivered during the preceding seasons.

## Reputation

Zemdro is viewed as one of the leading tacticians of his generation. His progression with Merino and subsequent back-to-back championships at Sporting give him three league titles across two major clubs. At the same time, the manner of his move from Merino ensures that one of his greatest achievements remains inseparable from the hostility surrounding his departure.
