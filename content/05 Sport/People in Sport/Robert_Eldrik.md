# Robert Eldrik

**Robert Eldrik** is an Agactan soccer manager and former player from Abeldrip. He is the manager of [[Setroburg Olimpik]], whom he led to the [[2025–26 Agactan Soccer Season|2025–26 Agactan Division 1 championship]].

## Playing career

Eldrik began his playing career with his hometown club, [[OSK Abeldrip]]. He subsequently moved to [[Setroburg Olimpik]], where he spent the central part of his career, before returning to Abeldrip for his final years as a player.

That background gave him close ties to both clubs that would later define his managerial career: the smaller prairie club where he was raised and one of the traditional powers of Agactan soccer.

## Managerial career

### OSK Abeldrip

Eldrik began his senior managerial career with [[OSK Abeldrip]]. In 2021–22, his first season in charge, he led the club to promotion from Division 2. Abeldrip then finished ninth in Division 1 in 2022–23, securing survival in their first season back in the top flight.

His work at Abeldrip established a clear early progression: promotion followed by consolidation on a limited budget.

### Setroburg Olimpik

Eldrik was appointed by [[Setroburg Olimpik]] ahead of the 2023–24 season.

His first campaign produced a third-place league finish and victory in the [[Agactan Cup]]. Setroburg improved to second in 2024–25 while also enjoying a strong run in the [[ACFA Trophy]]. In 2025–26, his third season in charge, Eldrik completed the progression by leading Setroburg to the Division 1 championship.

## Reputation

Eldrik's rise has been unusually orderly: promotion, survival, a move to a major club, third place and a cup, second place, and finally the championship. Setroburg's patience across that three-year build was rewarded with the league title.
