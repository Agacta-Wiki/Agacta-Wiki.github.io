# Rivalries in Agactan sport

Sporting rivalries in Agacta are primarily based on competition between the nineteen provincial franchises of the [[Agactan Sports Commission]] (ASC).

Unlike many European sporting systems, Agacta has few major same-city derbies at the professional level. This reflects the ASC's longstanding policy of promoting unified provincial representation and avoiding multiple elite franchises within the same city or province.

As the same franchise identities are used across multiple sports, major rivalries frequently extend beyond a single sporting code. A rivalry between two franchises may be contested in [[Agactan Rules Football]], soccer, rugby league, basketball and other ASC competitions.

---

# Major national rivalries

## The Classic

**[[Treslio Thunder]] v [[Setroburg Sentinels]]**

The Classic is one of the principal rivalries in Agactan sport and is contested between Treslio and Setroburg.

It is widely regarded as one of the country's most prestigious sporting fixtures and extends across multiple ASC sports.

---

## The Super Grande

**[[Treslio Thunder]] v [[Nleto Knights]]**

The Super Grande is the rivalry between Treslio and Nleto.

Alongside the Classic and the Monumental, it forms part of the group of major national fixtures involving Agacta's three traditional sporting powers.

---

## The Monumental

**[[Nleto Knights]] v [[Setroburg Sentinels]]**

The Monumental is the rivalry between Nleto and Setroburg.

Together with the Classic and the Super Grande, it forms one of the three principal national rivalry fixtures in Agactan sport.

# Secondary national rivalries

## Baby Classic

The term **Baby Classic** is most commonly used for fixtures involving [[Setroburg Sentinels]] and a rising challenger to the traditional elite, particularly [[Merino Mariners]] or [[Orientoport Owls]].

The term is generally used when the challenger is competing near the top of a major ASC competition, giving the fixture greater national significance.

---

## Baby Super Grande

The term **Baby Super Grande** may be applied to a fixture involving [[Treslio Thunder]] and an emerging challenger when the competitive circumstances resemble the national importance of the Super Grande on a smaller scale.

Its use is generally dependent on the form and status of the teams involved.

---

## Baby Monumental

The term **Baby Monumental** may similarly be used for fixtures involving [[Nleto Knights]] and a rising challenger when the match acquires wider national significance.

Like other "Baby" rivalry terms, its use is contextual rather than permanent.

---

# Rivalry culture

Agactan sporting rivalries differ from many traditional European derbies because the professional franchise system was deliberately designed to minimise internal provincial divisions.

The ASC historically opposed the development of multiple elite franchises within the same city or province, arguing that professional sport should strengthen community and provincial identity rather than divide local populations.

As a result, Agacta's largest rivalries generally developed between provinces, major cities and geographic regions rather than between neighbouring clubs within the same urban area.

The system has often been contrasted with cities such as Milan or Glasgow, where support for competing clubs forms a major part of local identity.
