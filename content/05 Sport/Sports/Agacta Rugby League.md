# Rugby league in Agacta

**Rugby league** is a major professional sport in Agacta and is organised by the [[Agactan Sports Commission]] (ASC).

Like other major ASC sports, rugby league operates through Agacta's provincial franchise system. All nineteen main provincial franchises field professional rugby league teams, with promotion and relegation determining which franchises compete in the top division.

The sport is played across mainland Agacta and the country's overseas territories and forms part of the wider multi-sport culture surrounding the nineteen provincial franchises.

---

# Organisation

Rugby league in Agacta is administered by the [[Agactan Sports Commission]], which is responsible for the organisation of professional competitions, development structures and the wider domestic game.

The same franchise identities used in other major Agactan sports are used in rugby league. Supporters therefore follow teams such as the [[Treslio Thunder]], [[Kalisto Falcons]] and [[Nleto Knights]] as part of broader multi-sport provincial institutions rather than independent rugby league clubs.

All nineteen principal provincial franchises operate professional rugby league teams.

---

# League system

The highest level of Agactan rugby league consists of a **10-team top division**.

The remaining principal provincial franchises compete below the top flight within the wider ASC competition structure.

Promotion and relegation operate between divisions. The lowest-placed main franchise in the top division may be relegated and replaced by a promoted main provincial franchise from the division below.

As in other ASC sports, minor franchises may participate within the lower national divisions but are not eligible for promotion to the top division.

# Competition format

The top division consists of ten franchises competing during the regular season.

Following the regular season, the leading teams advance to the postseason, which determines the national champion.

The championship trophy is the **[[Kopa de Metcalfe]]**.

# Broadcasting

Agactan rugby league is broadcast nationally across several television networks.

## Rugby League television schedule

The top-level [[Rugby League in Agacta|Agactan Rugby League]] schedule consists of five principal fixture slots spread across Saturday, Sunday and Monday.

| Day | Kick-off | Selection | Broadcaster |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Saturday | 14:00* | Pick 5 | [[Invictus Sport]] |
| Saturday | 16:00 | Pick 3 | [[Star Network]] |
| Saturday | 18:00 | Pick 1 | [[AgaSat Sport]] |
| Sunday | 12:00 | Pick 4 | [[AgaSat Sport]] |
| Monday | 21:00 | Pick 2 | [[Invictus Sport]] |

**AgaSat Sport** holds first pick of each round, broadcasting its principal match at **18:00 on Saturday**. It also holds the Sunday 12:00 package, carrying fourth pick.

**Invictus Sport** holds second pick, shown in the prominent **Monday 21:00** slot, as well as the Saturday 14:00 package carrying fifth pick.

The **Saturday 16:00 fixture** is shown free-to-air by [[Star Network]] and carries third pick. This ensures that one Rugby League match from each round remains available on free television.

Rugby League scheduling is designed partly around the wider [[Agacta Sports Commission]] broadcasting calendar, particularly to minimise clashes with [[Agactan Rules]] and other major domestic sports.

# Supporter culture

Rugby league shares the wider supporter culture of the ASC franchise system.

Matches are commonly played within provincial [[Sports Campuses in Agacta|sports campuses]], where rugby league supporters use the same bars, outdoor gathering areas and franchise facilities associated with other sports.

Because franchise loyalty extends across sporting codes, rugby league supporters commonly identify with the wider provincial franchise rather than with an independent rugby-specific club.

A supporter of the Kalisto Falcons, for example, may follow the Falcons in rugby league alongside the franchise's teams in soccer, [[Agactan Rules Football]], basketball and other ASC competitions.

---

# Relationship with other sports

Rugby league exists within Agacta's highly integrated multi-sport system.

Unlike countries where rugby league clubs operate independently from soccer, basketball or other sporting organisations, Agactan rugby league teams form part of the same provincial franchises used throughout ASC sport.

This structure means rugby league shares:

- Franchise identities
- Colours and branding
- Sports campuses
- Supporter cultures
- Development structures
- Administrative resources

with other sports played by the same provincial franchise.
