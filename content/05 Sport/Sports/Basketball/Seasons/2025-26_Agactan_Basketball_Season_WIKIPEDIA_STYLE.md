# 2025--26 Agactan Basketball Season

The **2025--26 Agactan Basketball Season** was the national professional
basketball season in Agacta. The season comprised the East, North, West
and South Cups, with results from the four competitions forming the
Championship table. The eight highest-ranked franchises advanced to the
Agactan Basketball Championship playoffs.

**Treslio Thunder** won the national championship, defeating **Savtrio
Tropicbirds 4--2** in the Championship Final. Treslio also won the West
and South Cups and finished first in the Championship table with 60
points.

## Teams

Nineteen professional franchises participated during the season. Sixteen
competed in Division 1 at any one time, with three professional
franchises competing in Division 2 alongside semi-professional teams.

The professional Division 2 franchises at the beginning of the season
were **Tarbo Tigers, Tlelmund Seals and Nlastromund Wolves**.

Promotion and relegation took place after each Cup.

## East Cup

Savtrio Tropicbirds won the East Cup, defeating Setroburg Lynxes in the
final. Kalisto Kestrels and Riala Rapids reached the semi-finals.

### Promotion and relegation

Tarbo Tigers won Division 2 and were promoted. Tlelmund Seals finished
as the professional runner-up but lost the promotion/relegation playoff.

Abeldrip were relegated.

### Promotion and relegation

The four Division 1 teams in the relegation stage were Aquatrip Sunsets,
Tarbo Tigers, Bantro Bison and Ulos Blizzard. Tarbo defeated Ulos and
Aquatrip defeated Bantro. Bantro then defeated Ulos, leaving Ulos
relegated.

Kalisto Kestrels won Division 2 and earned automatic promotion.
Montanomund Mountaineers finished as runner-up and defeated Bantro in
the promotion/relegation playoff.

Montanomund were promoted and Bantro were relegated.

Orientoport and Stototrip finished level on 37 points. They could not be
separated by Cup wins, Cup final appearances, Cup semi-final appearances
or number of Cups played in Division 1.

A neutral-site playoff was held in Merino, which **Orientoport won**,
giving them the sixth seed and Stototrip the seventh.

Tarbo claimed the eighth and final playoff place with 32 points, one
point ahead of Riala.
