# Basketball in Agacta

**Basketball in Agacta** is organised nationally through \[\[ASC
Basketball\]\] and is played through a professional and
semi-professional competition structure. The top level of the sport is
unusual in that the national season is not based around a single
conventional league campaign. Instead, professional franchises compete
across four historic Cup competitions, with their results accumulating
toward qualification for the season-ending \[[Agactan Basketball
Championship](#agactan-basketball-championship)\].

Agacta has **19 professional basketball franchises**. Sixteen compete in
Division 1 at any one time, while the remaining professional teams
compete alongside leading semi-professional sides in Division 2.
Promotion and relegation can occur after each of the four Cups, allowing
the composition of Division 1 to change during the season.

The national Championship is the principal domestic title.

## Organisation

The sport is administered by \[\[ASC Basketball\]\].

The modern national system combines professional basketball,
semi-professional competition, the four historic regional Cups and the
national Championship.

The four major Cups are:

1.  \[\[East Cup\]\]
2.  \[\[North Cup\]\]
3.  \[\[West Cup\]\]
4.  \[\[South Cup\]\]

Although associated with the four regional divisions of ASC Basketball,
the Cups are national competitions rather than tournaments restricted to
teams from their respective regions.

Each Cup remains administered by the relevant regional division and
retains its own identity and history.

## Divisional structure

### Division 1

Division 1 contains **16 professional franchises**.

Its membership is not fixed for an entire season. Promotion and
relegation take place after each Cup, meaning a franchise can move
between Divisions 1 and 2 during the same Championship campaign.

### Division 2

Division 2 contains **eight teams**.

It normally consists of:

-   the three professional franchises outside Division 1
-   five leading semi-professional franchises

Professional and semi-professional teams therefore compete together in
Division 2, although semi-professional teams are not eligible for
promotion into the professional Division 1.

Professional franchises competing in Division 2 continue to earn a
limited number of points toward the national Championship.

### Lower divisions

A semi-professional structure exists below Division 2, including
Division 3.

Semi-professional teams have their own promotion, relegation and points
hierarchy. Their results within that structure do not count toward the
professional Championship standings.

## National season

The national season consists of five principal stages:

1.  **East Cup**
2.  **North Cup**
3.  **West Cup**
4.  **South Cup**
5.  **Agactan Basketball Championship playoffs**

The four Cups serve two purposes.

Each is an independent trophy competition with its own champion, while
finishing positions also award points toward the season-long
Championship standings.

After all four Cups, the eight professional franchises with the most
Championship points qualify for the Championship playoffs.

For the complete competition system, see \[\[Agactan Basketball
Season\]\].

## The four Cups

The four-Cup structure developed from competitions that existed before
the creation of the modern national basketball system.

The regional divisions of ASC Basketball historically organised their
own open tournaments. These competitions were open to teams from across
Agacta rather than being limited to the region organising them.

When a national competition structure emerged, the four tournaments were
retained and incorporated into the national season.

### East Cup and West Cup

The East and West Cups use:

-   two Division 1 groups of eight
-   a single round robin within each group
-   six knockout qualifiers

The two group winners advance directly to the semifinals, while the
second- and third-place teams enter the quarterfinals.

### North Cup and South Cup

The North and South Cups use:

-   four Division 1 groups of four
-   a double round robin within each group
-   eight quarterfinalists

The top two teams from each group qualify for the knockout stage.

### South Cup seeding

The South Cup differs from the other Cups because its draw is seeded
according to the cumulative Championship standings after the first three
competitions.

The 16 current Division 1 teams are separated into four pots according
to their Championship positions, with one team from each pot drawn into
each group.

## Championship points

Professional franchises accumulate Championship points according to
their finishing position in each Cup.

Cup champions receive **20 points**, with progressively smaller awards
for runners-up, semifinalists and lower finishing bands.

The exact values differ slightly between the East/West and North/South
Cups because the proportion of teams qualifying for their respective
quarterfinals is different.

Division 2 professional franchises can also earn Championship points,
although the available totals are deliberately lower than those
available through strong Division 1 performances.

The points accumulated across all four Cups determine the final
Championship standings.

## Agactan Basketball Championship

The \[[Agactan Basketball
Championship](#agactan-basketball-championship)\] determines the
national champion.

The top eight teams in the cumulative standings qualify and are seeded
from first to eighth.

The playoff bracket begins:

-   1st vs 8th
-   2nd vs 7th
-   3rd vs 6th
-   4th vs 5th

The quarterfinals are played as **best-of-five series**.

The semifinals and Championship Final are **best-of-seven series**.

Winning one of the four Cups does not provide automatic qualification
for the Championship. A Cup winner must still finish inside the overall
top eight after all four competitions.

The winner of the Championship Final is recognised as the **Agactan
national basketball champion**.

## Promotion and relegation

Promotion and relegation form an active part of the national basketball
season rather than taking place only after the Championship.

After each Cup, the lowest-performing Division 1 franchises enter
relegation games while eligible professional teams from Division 2
compete for promotion.

A Division 2 professional champion can earn automatic promotion, while
an eligible runner-up can receive a promotion/relegation playoff.

Because semi-professional teams cannot enter Division 1, the exact
number of available promotion and relegation places can vary depending
on which teams reach the top positions in Division 2.

This creates the possibility of a professional franchise being relegated
during one Cup and returning to Division 1 later in the same season.

## Championship tiebreakers

Teams level on Championship points are separated by:

1.  most Cup wins
2.  most Cup final appearances
3.  most Cup semifinal appearances
4.  most Cups played in Division 1
5.  a neutral-site playoff

Quarterfinal appearances are not used because the East/West and
North/South quarterfinal qualification systems are not equivalent.

## Altur competition

Agactan basketball teams can also compete in the \[\[Altur Champions
Cup\]\].

The Altur Champions Cup is a knockout continental competition. It is
separate from the domestic Championship system and does not replace the
four-Cup national season.

Within Agactan basketball, the domestic national Championship is
regarded as the principal title.
