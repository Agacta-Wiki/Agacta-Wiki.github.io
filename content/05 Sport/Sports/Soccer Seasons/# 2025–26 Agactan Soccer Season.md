# 2025–26 Agactan Soccer Season

The **2025–26 Agactan soccer season** was the [number] season of the modern Agactan league system.

[[Setroburg Olympik]] won the Division 1 championship, reclaiming the title from [[Sporting Treslio]]. It was Setroburg's second championship in four seasons.

[[Inter Stototrip]] won Division 2 and earned promotion to the top flight, replacing relegated [[Nlastromund RRK]].

In the regionalised third tier, [[Sporting Zogyo]] finished first in Group A. [[Tleve Atleto]] won Group B and secured promotion to Division 2.

## Division 3

Division 3 was divided geographically into two groups:

- **Group A:** South, East and the tropical territories
- **Group B:** West, North and the polar territories

Developmental clubs belonging to the same franchise were permitted to compete against one another in Division 3. Promotion remained subject to the league's restrictions on multiple teams controlled by the same franchise.

## Developmental-team system

Developmental teams in Agactan soccer represent separate towns within a senior club's county franchise. They have their own grounds, facilities, coaching staffs and local supporter bases.

For example, residents of Yojo may support [[Yojo Sporting]] as their town club while also supporting [[Sporting Treslio]] as the wider county side.

### Competition rules

- Teams belonging to the same franchise cannot compete together in Division 1 or Division 2.
- A franchise's B and C teams cannot both compete in Division 2.
- Multiple teams from the same franchise may compete together in Division 3.
- If a senior team is relegated into a division occupied by one of its developmental teams, the developmental team is automatically demoted.
- B-team and C-team designations are not permanently attached to particular clubs.
- If one developmental club earns promotion while another is relegated, the franchise decides which club will occupy its Division 2 place.
- Developmental clubs competing in Division 3 are operated separately.
- Matches between clubs belonging to the same franchise are fully competitive.
