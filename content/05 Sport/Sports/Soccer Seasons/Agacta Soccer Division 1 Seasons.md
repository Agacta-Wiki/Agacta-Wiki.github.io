# Agactan Division 1 Historical Standings, 2004–05 to 2025–26

This article records the final standings of the [[Agactan Division 1]] from the 2004–05 season through the 2025–26 season.

Only finishing positions and confirmed promotion or relegation information are included. Points, match records and goal differences have not yet been established.

## Championship honours

The established roll of honour currently covers 100 seasons, from 1926–27 through 2025–26. The league dates from approximately 1924, but champions for 1924–25 and 1925–26 have not yet been added to the record. Consequently, the totals below cover the complete supplied list rather than those two presently unrecorded seasons.
