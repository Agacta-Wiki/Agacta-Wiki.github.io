# SLC Arena

## Overview

The **SLC Arena** is the permanent home of **Super League Combat (SLC)** in **Treslio, Agacta**.

The venue occupies part of a former airport complex that was converted into a wrestling arena, production base, training facility, office complex, and headquarters for the promotion.

The unusual setting is one of the most important parts of SLC's identity. Rather than resembling a conventional sports arena or polished television studio, the SLC Arena has an industrial, underground atmosphere that reflects the promotion's rough-edged and intimate presentation.

## Origins

The site was originally part of an airport complex in Treslio before being abandoned and later acquired for redevelopment.

When SLC was established, the location was selected partly because it provided enough space for an arena, production areas, offices, training facilities, storage, and backstage infrastructure within a single complex.

The choice also helped give SLC a visual identity immediately distinct from larger wrestling promotions.

Rather than attempting to hide the building's previous use, much of the airport character was deliberately retained.

## Layout

The SLC Arena is built around a relatively compact wrestling space designed to keep spectators close to the ring.

The venue prioritises atmosphere and crowd intimacy over maximum capacity. Fans are positioned close enough to the action that crowd reactions are clearly audible and wrestlers can interact directly with spectators during entrances and matches.

The wider airport complex contains several additional areas used by SLC, including:

- production and television facilities;
- locker rooms;
- training and conditioning areas;
- creative and production offices;
- interview locations;
- equipment storage;
- medical and treatment rooms;
- loading and staging areas;
- rehearsal and preparation spaces.

Parts of the former airport infrastructure remain visible throughout the complex.

## Presentation

SLC deliberately avoids making the arena look completely polished.

Concrete surfaces, exposed structural elements, industrial corridors, old airport architecture, and repurposed spaces contribute to the venue's visual identity.

The promotion regularly uses different parts of the building for backstage interviews and storylines rather than restricting all non-wrestling material to identical interview sets.

As a result, areas such as corridors, loading zones, former terminal spaces, stairwells, service areas, and hangar-like sections can become familiar locations to regular viewers.

This helps create the impression that SLC exists within a broader wrestling world rather than simply inside a television set.

## Atmosphere

The SLC Arena is designed to produce a cult, underground atmosphere.

The relatively small size of the venue allows regular fans to become familiar faces and contributes to the sense of a close relationship between the promotion, wrestlers, and audience.

SLC supporters often describe the appeal of attending shows in terms of having discovered something outside mainstream wrestling.

The venue's rough-edged appearance is considered a feature rather than a flaw.

Even as SLC grows, the promotion has generally resisted attempts to make the arena resemble the highly polished presentation of larger international wrestling companies.

## The Airport as Part of SLC Lore

The abandoned-airport setting gradually became part of SLC's mythology.

Different areas of the complex have been associated with particular wrestlers, factions, interviews, confrontations, and backstage incidents.

This has allowed the building itself to function almost as a recurring character within SLC programming.

The airport also reinforces SLC's broader identity as a promotion existing slightly outside the wrestling mainstream: unconventional, self-contained, intimate, and unpredictable.

## Headquarters

In addition to hosting SLC's main televised events, the complex serves as the company's operational headquarters.

SLC's president, creative team, production staff, and administrative staff work from offices within the site.

The centralisation of wrestling, production, creative, and business operations within the same complex contributes to the promotion's relatively close internal culture.

## Training

SLC also maintains training facilities within the airport complex.

These are used for wrestler preparation, conditioning, rehearsals, match planning, and the development of newer talent.

The training area allows younger wrestlers and established performers to work in close proximity to SLC's production and creative staff.

## Importance to SLC

The SLC Arena is considered inseparable from the promotion's identity.

While SLC may occasionally run larger venues or special events elsewhere, the Treslio airport complex remains its spiritual home.

For many supporters, the venue represents the qualities that distinguish SLC from larger promotions: intimacy, unpredictability, rough edges, accessibility, and the feeling of belonging to a wrestling culture that has grown organically rather than being manufactured around a conventional arena.
