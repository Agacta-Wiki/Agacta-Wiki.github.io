# SLC Roster

## Overview

**Super League Combat (SLC)** maintains a relatively compact active roster so that regular viewers can remain familiar with most performers and their current direction.

The roster combines:

- wrestlers developed or elevated within SLC;
- established industry veterans;
- wrestlers whose careers are being repositioned or revived;
- specialist performers representing different wrestling styles;
- a smaller group of recurring enhancement talent.

SLC deliberately avoids maintaining an excessively large roster. Wrestlers are expected to have a recognisable role, goal, rivalry, or developmental direction rather than disappearing from programming for long periods without explanation.

## Main Event and Upper Card

### Matt Riddle

**Matt Riddle** is one of SLC's leading wrestlers and a central member of **The Industry Standard**.

Riddle presents himself as an elite combat athlete and believes that technical skill, physical legitimacy, and competitive superiority place him above most of the roster.

He became the first **SLC World Champion** by defeating **Rob Van Dam** at **Ignition**.

Riddle is often willing to win cleanly and uses those victories to reinforce his belief that he does not need shortcuts to dominate SLC.

### Dante Navarro

**Dante Navarro**, known as **Skyward**, is one of SLC's most highly regarded younger wrestlers.

He is positioned as a long-term future ace of the promotion, with his story focused on development, setbacks, confidence, and gradually proving that he belongs at the highest level.

Navarro has developed a close relationship with veteran **Rob Van Dam**, who increasingly acts as a mentor.

### Simon Vale

**Simon Vale**, known as **The Technician**, is a technical and amateur-wrestling specialist and a member of **The Industry Standard**.

Vale is intensely proud of his wrestling ability and views technical excellence as the purest form of professional wrestling.

Although initially positioned as one of Riddle's closest allies, Vale is also ambitious enough to eventually see himself as capable of leading the faction.

### Velociraptor

**Velociraptor** is a high-flying powerhouse with a strong reputation from the independent wrestling scene.

He became the first **SLC Challenge Champion** at Ignition.

His style combines athleticism, speed, and power, making him one of the most versatile wrestlers on the roster.

Velociraptor is expected to remain a significant singles competitor and a potential future World Championship contender.

### Kevin Nash

**Kevin Nash** is one of SLC's most experienced veterans.

Nash remains a major presence despite being later in his career and is frequently portrayed as a wrestler whose reputation, intelligence, and political instincts make him dangerous even when he is not directly involved in a championship programme.

He has been associated with Matt Riddle and The Industry Standard while maintaining his own veteran identity.

### Rob Van Dam

**Rob Van Dam** is a veteran babyface and one of SLC's most recognisable established wrestlers.

RVD combines his own championship ambitions with an increasing role as a mentor to younger wrestlers, particularly Dante Navarro.

His experience and unconventional style make him an important bridge between SLC's veteran and emerging generations.

### Sheamus

**Sheamus O'Shaughnessy** is an internationally experienced veteran who joined SLC after a highly successful career elsewhere.

He is presented as a major competitive threat rather than simply as a nostalgia signing.

Sheamus brings significant credibility to SLC through his championship experience and reputation as a physical, hard-hitting wrestler.

## Upper Mid-Card and Featured Singles Wrestlers

### Yoon Tae-Seok

**Yoon Tae-Seok** is a Korean strong-style wrestler and member of **The Industry Standard**.

Yoon represents the faction's strong-style element and combines disciplined striking, physical intensity, and technical ability.

A major early victory over Dante Navarro established him as a serious upper-card threat.

### Quo Zabay

**Quo Zabay**, known as **The Diplomat**, is the leader of **The Delegation**.

Zabay presents himself as a corrupt and calculating political operator who prefers influence, access, negotiation, and manipulation to direct confrontation.

He is willing to wrestle when necessary but frequently sends members of The Delegation to act on his behalf.

### Demolition Jones

**Demolition Jones**, known as **The Mad Man of the People**, is a chaotic hardcore specialist and cult favourite.

He is known for bright sky-blue hair, a brown goatee, and an unpredictable personality.

Although closely associated with hardcore wrestling, Jones is determined to prove that he can also succeed in standard wrestling matches.

His popularity and unconventional style have gradually pushed him toward the main-event scene.

### George Pembrook

**George Pembrook**, also known as **Captain Fantasy**, is an eccentric English high flyer with the personality of a flamboyant rock star.

Pembrook combines elaborate entrances, unusual charisma, and spectacular offence.

He is positioned as a long-term breakout star whose popularity can grow organically before he becomes a regular main-event wrestler.

### Desmond Boyce

**Desmond Boyce**, known as **The World's Greatest Showman**, is a polished and highly self-confident heel.

Boyce believes wrestling should be presented with sophistication and star power and frequently expresses contempt for wrestlers he considers too rough, eccentric, or rooted in indie wrestling culture.

His confidence often exceeds his current achievements, allowing his character to remain prominent even when his win-loss record is inconsistent.

### Giovanni Vinci

**Giovanni Vinci** is a polished European wrestler associated with **The Delegation**.

He brings a more refined and athletic presence to the faction and often acts as one of Quo Zabay's primary in-ring representatives.

### Mark Henry

**Mark Henry** is a veteran powerhouse associated with The Delegation.

He generally acts as the faction's heavy, giving Zabay access to a physically intimidating enforcer.

Henry can also appear as a special attraction or veteran measuring-stick opponent.

## Tag Teams and Factions

### The Industry Standard

**The Industry Standard** is one of SLC's most important factions.

Its core members are:

- Matt Riddle
- Simon Vale
- Yoon Tae-Seok
- Kevin Nash

The faction believes its members represent elite professional wrestling and combat-sport ability.

Riddle serves as the central figure, while Vale represents technical wrestling, Yoon represents strong style, and Nash adds veteran experience and political intelligence.

### The Delegation

**The Delegation** is led by Quo Zabay.

Current members and associates include:

- Quo Zabay
- Champagne Syndicate
- Giovanni Vinci
- Mark Henry

The faction is built around influence, access, favours, negotiation, and career advancement rather than simple physical domination.

### Champagne Syndicate

**Champagne Syndicate** consists of:

- Clark Montrose
- Eliot Voss

They are a polished, old-school heel tag team and the first **SLC Tag Team Champions**.

The team is associated with The Delegation.

### Pavement Cartel

**Pavement Cartel** consists of:

- Behemoth
- Trayvon Steel
- Dee Woodland

The group combines street credibility with contrasting wrestling styles.

Behemoth provides heavyweight power, Trayvon Steel brings cruiserweight athleticism and singles potential, and Dee Woodland is primarily a tag-team specialist.

### Rock and Rap Connection

**Rock and Rap Connection** consists of:

- Alfie Rowe
- T-Style

The team developed from the clash between Alfie's Britpop-inspired rock-star personality and T-Style's hip-hop identity.

Their contrasting personalities are central to the act.

They are managed by **Music Biz Bob**.

### The Vertex

**The Vertex** is the veteran babyface team of:

- Justin Gabriel
- Heath Slater

The team revives the name and identity of an earlier celebrated stable while functioning in SLC primarily as a respected veteran tag-team act.

Their presentation is nostalgic without attempting to recreate the past completely.

They use the catchphrase:

> "You are either Vertex or against us."

The team is generally positioned around the upper mid-card and tag-team title scene, although Gabriel remains capable of challenging for major singles championships.

**Ryback** may occasionally be associated with the team.

### Bannons in Pyjamas

**Bannons in Pyjamas** consists of:

- Jimmy Bannon
- Joe Bannon

They are a recurring comedy and enhancement tag team known for banana-themed presentation and unexpectedly strong fighting spirit.

## Other Active Wrestlers

### Behemoth

**Behemoth** is the powerhouse member of Pavement Cartel.

Although primarily associated with the group, he also competes regularly in singles matches.

### Trayvon Steel

**Trayvon Steel** is the most athletic member of Pavement Cartel and has significant potential as a singles wrestler.

### Dee Woodland

**Dee Woodland** is primarily a tag-team specialist and a core member of Pavement Cartel.

### Alfie Rowe

**Alfie Rowe** is a Britpop-inspired wrestler with a strong rock-star persona.

He is one half of Rock and Rap Connection.

### T-Style

**T-Style** is a hip-hop-inspired wrestler and the other half of Rock and Rap Connection.

### Douglas Wright

**Douglas Wright** is a working-class babyface whose background as a plumber remains reflected in his wrestling presentation.

He began wrestling part-time while still working as a plumber and retained ring gear that closely resembles his former work uniform.

His finishing move is the **Pipe Slam**.

### Buck Harwood

**Buck Harwood** is a rough-edged lower-card bruiser.

His current presentation retains a working-man and plumber-inspired identity.

## Enhancement and Recurring Talent

SLC also uses a small number of non-contracted or lightly featured wrestlers who can appear when needed without requiring continuous storylines.

### Fabrizzio

**Fabrizzio** is an Italian-Agactan wrestler who began as a singing waiter in his family's Italian restaurant.

He continues to promote the family business and uses Italian folk music for his entrance.

### El Masko

**El Masko** is a local lower-card wrestler with a strong enthusiasm for lucha libre.

He is generally presented as an underdog.

### Barry Wales

**Barry Wales** is a red-haired lower-card wrestler who believes he may be distantly related to royalty.

### Jimmy and Joe Bannon

The Bannon brothers also appear regularly as enhancement talent when not featured as a formal tag-team act.

## Roster Philosophy

SLC does not expect every wrestler to appear on every episode or major event.

However, major and upper-midcard wrestlers are generally expected to remain visible enough that the audience understands their current direction.

Missing one major event is considered normal.

Repeatedly leaving an important wrestler without a storyline or television presence is avoided where possible.

The roster is intentionally kept small enough that viewers can understand who the wrestlers are, what they want, and where they currently stand within SLC.
