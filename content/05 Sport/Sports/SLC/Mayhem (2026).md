# Mayhem (2026)

**Mayhem (2026)** was a professional wrestling pay-per-view event produced by **Super League Combat (SLC)**. It took place on **22 August 2026** at the **SLC Arena** in **Treslio, Agacta**.

The event was held in front of a sell-out crowd of **1,500 spectators** and featured eight matches.

In the main event, **Matt Riddle** successfully defended the **SLC World Championship** against **Justin Gabriel**. Elsewhere, **Velociraptor** retained the **SLC Challenge Championship** against **Quo Zabay**, **Yoon Tae-Seok** defeated **Dante Navarro**, and **Demolition Jones** gained a victory over **Simon Vale**.

The event also featured victories for **George Pembrook**, **Desmond Boyce**, **Kevin Nash**, and **Pavement Cartel**.

## Background

Mayhem followed the events of **Ignition**, SLC's inaugural pay-per-view.

The main event featured **Matt Riddle** defending the SLC World Championship against **Justin Gabriel**.

Gabriel entered the title picture following a series of victories that elevated him into contention. His friendship and alliance with **Heath Slater** became an important part of the build, with Riddle repeatedly attempting to create tension between the pair.

Riddle argued that Gabriel had previously left Slater behind and attempted to exploit doubts about their loyalty to one another.

During the match, **Kevin Nash** attempted to interfere on Riddle's behalf. Slater stopped Nash from becoming involved, leaving the championship to be decided in the ring.

Despite losing this advantage, Riddle defeated Gabriel cleanly to retain the championship.

Another major programme involved **Dante Navarro** and **Yoon Tae-Seok**.

Navarro had scored a major victory over Kevin Nash at Ignition and entered Mayhem with considerable momentum. Yoon, however, defeated Navarro in a lengthy contest, giving him one of the most important wins of his SLC career and halting Navarro's rise.

The rivalry between **Simon Vale** and **Demolition Jones** also continued from Ignition.

Vale had defeated Jones at the previous event. At Mayhem, Jones gained revenge by defeating Vale, leaving the series tied at one victory each.

## Event

### George Pembrook vs. Mark Henry

The opening match featured **George Pembrook** against **Mark Henry**.

Henry used his size and strength to control long portions of the contest, while Pembrook relied on movement, speed, and his more unconventional offence.

Pembrook eventually scored the victory after **12 minutes and 27 seconds**.

The result was treated as a significant upset and gave Pembrook one of the biggest victories of his SLC career.

---

### Desmond Boyce vs. Behemoth

**Desmond Boyce** faced **Behemoth** of Pavement Cartel.

The match contrasted Boyce's polished and theatrical style with Behemoth's power-based offence.

Boyce won after **14 minutes and 41 seconds**.

The victory helped Boyce rebuild momentum following his loss to Velociraptor at Ignition.

---

### Kevin Nash vs. Rob Van Dam

Veterans **Kevin Nash** and **Rob Van Dam** met in the third match of the event.

The contest was relatively short compared with several matches elsewhere on the card.

Nash defeated Van Dam after **10 minutes and 13 seconds**.

The victory restored some momentum for Nash following his loss to Dante Navarro at Ignition.

---

### SLC Challenge Championship

**Velociraptor** defended the **SLC Challenge Championship** against **Quo Zabay**.

Zabay attempted to slow the pace and prevent Velociraptor from using his explosive offence.

Velociraptor ultimately retained the championship after **21 minutes and 37 seconds**.

The victory marked another successful step in Velociraptor's first reign as Challenge Champion.

---

### Simon Vale vs. Demolition Jones

**Simon Vale** faced **Demolition Jones** in a rematch from Ignition.

Vale had won their first encounter, but Jones entered Mayhem determined to prove that he could defeat Vale without relying solely on weapons or chaos.

The match lasted **17 minutes and 26 seconds**.

Jones won, evening their rivalry at one victory apiece.

---

### Pavement Cartel vs. Rock and Rap Connection

**Pavement Cartel** faced **Rock and Rap Connection**, consisting of **Alfie Rowe** and **T-Style**.

The match featured a contrast between Pavement Cartel's established teamwork and the more volatile personalities of Rock and Rap Connection.

Pavement Cartel won after **16 minutes and 38 seconds**.

The defeat contributed to the growing frustration surrounding Rock and Rap Connection and their increasingly antagonistic attitude.

---

### Yoon Tae-Seok vs. Dante Navarro

The penultimate match saw **Yoon Tae-Seok** face **Dante Navarro**.

Navarro entered the match with significant momentum following his victory over Kevin Nash at Ignition.

Yoon countered Navarro's aerial offence with a harder-hitting, more methodical style.

After **23 minutes and 46 seconds**, Yoon defeated Navarro with the **Cloudburst**.

The result was considered one of the most important victories of Yoon's SLC career and represented a major setback for Navarro.

---

### SLC World Championship

The main event featured **Matt Riddle** defending the **SLC World Championship** against **Justin Gabriel**.

Gabriel entered with **Heath Slater** in his corner, while **Kevin Nash** accompanied Riddle.

During the match, Nash attempted to interfere.

Slater stopped Nash, removing the possibility of outside involvement and allowing the match to continue without further interference.

Riddle ultimately defeated Gabriel cleanly after **22 minutes and 40 seconds** to retain the championship.

The result reinforced Riddle's presentation as a champion capable of winning without assistance, while Gabriel's performance established him as a credible main-event challenger.

## Reception

Mayhem received generally positive reviews.

Critics praised the event for continuing SLC's emphasis on meaningful wins and losses, while also noting that the card featured more storyline progression than Ignition.

The victories of **Yoon Tae-Seok**, **George Pembrook**, and **Demolition Jones** were frequently highlighted as examples of the promotion using results to change wrestlers' positions rather than relying on repetitive rematches.

The main event also received praise for allowing Matt Riddle to retain the World Championship cleanly despite being a heel.

Some criticism focused on the overall length of the show. With eight matches and several contests exceeding fifteen minutes, reviewers noted that Mayhem was considerably denser than Ignition.

**Yoon Tae-Seok vs. Dante Navarro** was generally considered the match of the night.

Reviewers praised the contrast between Navarro's speed and Yoon's harder-hitting style, as well as the significance of Yoon defeating one of SLC's fastest-rising wrestlers.

Pembrook's victory over Henry attracted attention less for technical match quality and more for its booking significance.

## Aftermath

Matt Riddle remained SLC World Champion and continued to establish himself as the central figure of **Industry Standard**.

Justin Gabriel remained in the upper-card picture despite the defeat, with his loyalty to Heath Slater strengthened by Slater preventing Nash from interfering.

Yoon Tae-Seok's victory over Dante Navarro significantly elevated his standing.

Navarro entered a period of frustration and self-doubt following the loss, eventually receiving guidance from **Rob Van Dam**.

Demolition Jones' victory over Simon Vale kept their rivalry alive and helped elevate Jones toward the World Championship picture.

Velociraptor continued his reign as SLC Challenge Champion, while Vale remained one of his most likely future challengers.

George Pembrook's victory over Mark Henry increased his credibility and eventually drew the attention of Kevin Nash.

The developments from Mayhem led directly into **Trial by Combat**, where several of the event's major rivalries continued.
