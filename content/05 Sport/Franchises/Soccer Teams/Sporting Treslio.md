# Sporting Treslio

**Sporting Treslio** is the soccer section of [[Treslio Thunder ASC Club]], the largest multi-sport club in [[Agacta]]. Based in the prosperous eastern capital of [[Treslio]], Sporting are the most successful club in the history of the [[Agactan Division 1]] and one of only two Agactan sides to have won the [[ACFA Altur Champion's Cup]].

The club are commonly known as **the Thunder**, **the Blues**, **the Super Blues** or simply **Sporting**. They play at the 65,000-capacity [[Dario Valto Stadium]], the largest dedicated soccer stadium in Agacta.

## Identity

Sporting retain the traditional soccer-style name used by the club before the wider Treslio organisation became commonly identified as the **Treslio Thunder**. Although the name differs from the teams representing Treslio in most other sports, Sporting share the same ownership, supporter base, colours, crest lineage and sports-campus identity.

Blue is the club's dominant colour and is traditionally accompanied by white trim. Black is occasionally introduced as a third colour, particularly on change kits. The size of the club, its consistent success and the confidence associated with the capital have made Sporting a symbol of Treslio both within Agacta and among the Agactan diaspora.

## History and status

Sporting have won **33 Agactan league championships**, more than any other club. Their first recorded title came in **1926–27**, while their most recent championship was secured in **2024–25**. Their total represents exactly one-third of the first 100 championships included in the established national roll of honour.

Alongside [[Setroburg Olympik]], Sporting are one of only two Agactan clubs to have conquered Altur's leading continental competition. They won the [[ACFA Altur Champion's Cup]] in **1971** and **1985**, victories which remain central to the club's self-image as Agacta's foremost international representative.

Further continental success followed in the [[ACFA Cup Winner's Cup]] in **1991** and the [[ACFA Trophy]] in **2002**. These achievements have helped Sporting develop a profile extending well beyond Agacta.

## Stadium and sports campus

Sporting play at [[Dario Valto Stadium]], a 65,000-capacity venue located within the wider [[Treslio Thunder Sports Campus]]. It is the largest soccer stadium in Agacta and regularly stages some of the country's most important domestic and continental fixtures.

The stadium forms only one part of the multi-sport complex used by Treslio Thunder. Bars, outdoor terraces, training facilities and venues for other ASC sports surround it, allowing the campus to remain active throughout the year.

On major matchdays, particularly ACFA nights and fixtures against Setroburg or Nleto, supporters begin gathering around the campus several hours before kickoff. The bars and outdoor barbecue areas become informal fan zones, with blue flags, scarves and banners extending well beyond the immediate approaches to the stadium.

### Stadium food

The best-known concession item at Dario Valto Stadium is the **Dario Double**, usually shortened by supporters to **a Dario**. It consists of two thin beef patties, mature blue cheese, charred onions and pepper-and-mustard sauce in a toasted bun, served in blue-and-white wrapping.

The older campus speciality is the **Thunder Sausage**, a thick pork-and-beef sausage seasoned with black pepper, garlic and smoked paprika. It is split, finished over charcoal and served in a crusty roll with grilled onions and sharp mustard.

The same sausage is served sliced as part of the larger barbecue plates sold at the campus bars. The outdoor cooking areas are collectively known as **the Thunder Grills**. Before major ACFA fixtures the grills open several hours before the stadium gates, and the smell of charcoal and smoked sausage is regarded as part of the traditional approach to Dario Valto Stadium.

## Support

Sporting are among the most widely supported teams in Agacta. Their support extends far beyond Treslio itself, and they have a particularly large following among Agactan communities elsewhere on Altur.

The club's ACFA campaigns often attract support from people who would normally oppose Sporting domestically. Many Agactans want to see an Agactan side win one of the major planetary competitions and consequently rally behind Sporting during the later rounds of continental tournaments.

Within Agacta, however, Sporting are probably the country's most disliked club. Their wealth, historic success and capital-city confidence produce particularly strong hostility in Setroburg, Nleto, Merino and Orientoport. Rival supporters frequently characterise Sporting as entitled or institutionally favoured, while Sporting supporters regard the resentment as an inevitable consequence of the club's stature.

The club's most prominent organised supporter groups are:

- **Blue Thunder**
- **Pordo 99**
- **The Capital Boys**

## Songs and matchday traditions

### Official anthem

The club's formal Ido-language anthem is **“La Blua Tondro”** (“The Blue Thunder”). It is a ceremonial and triumphant piece traditionally played as the teams prepare to enter the field. It is strongly associated with official club occasions, trophy presentations and commemorations of former players.

### Sempre Kun Vu

The song most closely identified with the terraces is **“Sempre Kun Vu”** (“Always With You”). It occupies a place in Sporting culture comparable to the great supporter anthems sung at major clubs elsewhere on Altur.

The song began as a sentimental Treslio harbour ballad about separation, loyalty and the families who waited for sailors to return home. Sporting supporters adopted it during the club's run to the **1971 ACFA Altur Champion's Cup**, allowing its maritime imagery to acquire a second meaning: wherever the team travelled, Treslio remained with them.

Before important matches, the opening passage is played over the stadium speakers while supporters raise their scarves. The recording is then lowered or stopped and the crowd carries the main refrain unaccompanied. The tradition is particularly powerful before ACFA knockout matches, the Classic and the Super Grande.

Unlike the official anthem, “Sempre Kun Vu” is not fundamentally a song about victory. Its importance lies in its promise of loyalty during both successful and unsuccessful periods, which has allowed it to remain emotionally significant despite Sporting's image as Agacta's dominant club.

## Mascot

Like the other Treslio Thunder teams, Sporting use **Thunder Man** as their mascot. Thunder Man is a large blue furry creature whose appearance has often been compared to Gottaguess from *Potamus Park*.

The mascot appears throughout the Treslio sports campus rather than belonging exclusively to the soccer section. This makes Thunder Man one of the most recognisable figures in Agactan sport, particularly among younger supporters.

## Rivalries

### The Classic — Setroburg Olympik

**Sporting Treslio v [[Setroburg Olympik]]** is known as **[[The Classic]]**. In most seasons it is the single biggest fixture in Agactan sport, between the country's two largest and most historically successful clubs.

The country effectively comes to a standstill when they meet. The buildup dominates sporting discussion throughout the week and frequently becomes a leading item on national news broadcasts rather than remaining confined to the sports pages.

The rivalry is strengthened by the contrast between the two cities. Treslio is the wealthy, seafaring eastern capital, historically associated with merchants, overseas trade and national power. Setroburg is the western second city: landlocked, although situated on a major lake, with a hard-working identity built around heavy industry.

For Sporting, Setroburg are the most direct challenge to their claim of being Agacta's leading club. For Setroburg, defeating Sporting represents both sporting supremacy and resistance to the political and commercial dominance of the capital.

### The Super Grande — Nleto

**Sporting Treslio v [[Nleto Distraktiva Klub]]** is known as **[[The Super Grande]]**. It is more or less level with the Classic as the biggest match in the country, with the relative importance of the two fixtures often depending on the strength and league position of the clubs involved.

Nleto bring a gritty, direct and proudly southern identity to the rivalry. The city is close to Setroburg in size and rejects any suggestion that Agacta has only two truly major urban or sporting powers. Nleto supporters regard their club and city as every bit as important as Treslio and Setroburg and approach the Super Grande as an opportunity to prove it.

The rivalry therefore carries a strong cultural contrast. Sporting represent the affluent, cosmopolitan and self-confident capital; Nleto present themselves as tough, practical, no-nonsense southerners who neither seek nor require approval from Treslio.

### Eastern Derby — Merino Jimnastika

The **[[Eastern Derby]]** is contested between Sporting and [[Merino Jimnastika]]. It is one of Sporting's principal regional fixtures and almost invariably the biggest match of Merino's season.

Merino approach the game with particular intensity, and the atmosphere is often described as electric. Sporting's greater wealth and larger national profile give Merino an obvious establishment rival, while Merino's ability to develop competitive teams ensures that the fixture cannot be treated as a ceremonial local derby.

The hostility has intensified under Sporting manager [[Walter Zemdro]], who left Merino immediately after guiding them to the 2021–22 championship. Zemdro is routinely booed on his return and is widely regarded as a traitor by Merino supporters.

### Port Derby — Orientoport RK

Sporting's rivalry with [[Orientoport RK]] is known as the **[[Port Derby]]**. Its importance has grown considerably with Orientoport's rise and its emergence as a league-winning force in the modern era.

The fixture brings together two confident eastern port cities with strong maritime identities. What was once viewed as a secondary regional rivalry has developed into one of Sporting's major matches, particularly when both clubs are competing near the top of the league.

For Orientoport, the game offers an opportunity to challenge the capital's assumption of eastern supremacy. Sporting increasingly regard Orientoport not simply as a neighbouring rival but as a credible threat to their domestic position.

## Walter Zemdro era

[[Walter Zemdro]] became Sporting manager in 2022, immediately after leading [[Merino Jimnastika]] to the 2021–22 championship. A sports-science graduate with a methodical reputation, he was appointed to restore Sporting to first place after they had finished fifth.

His first season produced a third-place finish and was considered disappointing. Sporting responded by winning consecutive championships in **2023–24** and **2024–25**. Setroburg reclaimed the title in 2025–26, with Sporting finishing second.

Despite delivering two championships, Zemdro entered 2026–27 under renewed pressure. At Sporting, success is measured against both immediate domestic supremacy and the club's historic ACFA achievements; finishing second can therefore be treated as failure rather than consolidation.
