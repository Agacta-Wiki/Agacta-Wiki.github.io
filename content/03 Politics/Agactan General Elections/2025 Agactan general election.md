# 2025 Agactan general election

The **2025 Agactan general election** was held on **___ 2025** to elect the 240 members of the [[Parliament of Agacta]].

The election resulted in the [[Constitution Party]] remaining the largest party in Parliament despite significant losses. Following coalition negotiations, the Constitution Party and [[Liberal Party]] agreed to renew their coalition government for a historic fourth consecutive term.

The election is chiefly remembered for the dramatic collapse of the [[Green Party]], the strong recovery of the [[Socialist Party]], and the continued growth of the [[Agacta Left Bloc]].

---

# Background

The election followed twelve years of Constitution Party-Liberal coalition government.

The campaign was dominated by the cost-of-living crisis, inflation and housing. Public debate also focused on healthcare, economic competitiveness and the future direction of climate policy.

The Green Party entered the election as the Official Opposition for the first time but faced criticism over its performance in the role. Meanwhile, the Socialist Party sought to rebuild after suffering two successive historic defeats, while the Agacta Left Bloc aimed to consolidate the gains it had made in recent years.

# Government formation

Although the Constitution Party lost eighteen seats, it remained the largest party in Parliament.

Following coalition negotiations, the Constitution Party and Liberal Party agreed to renew their coalition government with a combined total of **134 seats**.

The President subsequently invited the leader of the Constitution Party to form the next government, making it the first party in the history of the modern republic to lead four consecutive governments.

---

# Analysis

The election produced one of the largest political realignments in recent Agactan history.

The Constitution Party remained comfortably the largest party despite suffering significant losses, while the Liberal Party enjoyed one of its strongest performances in decades, enabling the governing coalition to retain a parliamentary majority.

The Socialist Party recorded a substantial recovery, gaining twenty-nine seats and returning to second place after eight years in opposition. Although still well below the levels of support it had traditionally enjoyed during the twentieth century, the result was widely interpreted as evidence that the party's long period of decline had begun to reverse.

The Green Party suffered one of the worst defeats in its history, losing thirty-one seats after a single parliamentary term as Official Opposition. Many analysts attributed the result to an ineffective period in opposition, combined with voters prioritising economic issues over environmental policy.

The Agacta Left Bloc continued its steady growth, doubling its parliamentary representation and establishing itself as a significant force on the political left.

---

# Legacy

The 2025 election confirmed the Constitution Party as the dominant governing force in contemporary Agactan politics, while delivering the first fourth consecutive term for any governing party since the establishment of the modern republic.

The election also marked the beginning of a new balance on the left of Agactan politics. The Socialist Party re-established itself as the principal opposition party, the Green Party lost much of the momentum it had built over the previous decade, and the Agacta Left Bloc emerged as an increasingly influential parliamentary force.

Many political commentators described the result as the beginning of a new five-party era, with electoral competition becoming more fragmented than at any previous point in the republic's history.
