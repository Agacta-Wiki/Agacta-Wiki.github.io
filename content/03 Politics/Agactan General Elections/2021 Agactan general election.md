# 2021 Agactan general election

The **2021 Agactan general election** was held on **___ 2021** to elect the 240 members of the [[Parliament of Agacta]].

The election resulted in a third consecutive victory for the [[Constitution Party]], which increased its parliamentary representation to a record 111 seats. Following coalition negotiations, the Constitution Party and [[Liberal Party]] renewed their coalition government for a third consecutive term.

The election is chiefly remembered for the [[Green Party]] becoming the **Official Opposition** for the first time in its history, narrowly overtaking the [[Socialist Party]] by a single seat.

---

# Background

The election followed four years of Constitution Party-Liberal coalition government.

The governing coalition campaigned on its record of economic management, political stability and continued investment in public services. The campaign was dominated by the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic, economic recovery and healthcare policy.

The Green Party sought to build upon its breakthrough performance in 2017, while the Socialist Party attempted to halt its long electoral decline. The Agacta Left Bloc also hoped to regain momentum after suffering losses at the previous election.

# Government formation

Following the election, the Constitution Party and Liberal Party agreed to renew their coalition government, holding a combined total of **141 seats**.

The President subsequently invited the leader of the Constitution Party to form the next government.

---

# Analysis

The election reinforced the Constitution Party's position as Agacta's dominant governing party. Winning 111 seats, it achieved its strongest parliamentary result since the establishment of the modern republic.

The Green Party narrowly became the Official Opposition for the first time, despite losing a single seat compared with the previous election. The result reflected the continued decline of the Socialist Party rather than further Green gains.

The Socialist Party suffered another disappointing election, falling to third place for the first time in modern Agactan history. Although its losses were smaller than those experienced in 2017, the result intensified debate about the party's long-term future.

The Liberal Party lost eight seats but comfortably retained its role as the Constitution Party's preferred coalition partner.

Meanwhile, the Agacta Left Bloc more than doubled its parliamentary representation, signalling a modest recovery after its poor performance in 2017.

---

# Legacy

The 2021 election marked the high-water mark of the Constitution Party's electoral dominance.

It also represented a historic breakthrough for the Green Party, which became the Official Opposition for the first time and appeared to establish itself as Agacta's principal party of the centre-left.

However, the election also hinted at changing dynamics on the left of Agactan politics. While the Greens occupied the position of Official Opposition, the Agacta Left Bloc began to recover, laying the foundations for the significant political realignment that would follow at the 2025 general election.
