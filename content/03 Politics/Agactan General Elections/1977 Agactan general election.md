# 1977 Agactan general election

The **1977 Agactan general election** was held on **___ 1977** to elect the 240 members of the [[Parliament of Agacta]].

Although the [[Socialist Party]] lost seats and fell short of an overall majority, it remained the largest party in Parliament. Following negotiations, the [[Liberal Party]] agreed to enter government with the Socialists, returning a centre-left coalition to office.

---

# Background

The election followed four years of Socialist majority government after the party's return to office in 1973.

The campaign centred on the government's economic record, public finances and the future direction of Agacta's development, with both major parties seeking to convince voters they were best placed to lead the country through the next parliamentary term.

# Government formation

The Socialist Party remained the largest party but lost its parliamentary majority.

Following coalition negotiations, the Socialist Party and Liberal Party agreed to form a government with a combined total of **148 seats**.

The President subsequently invited the leader of the Socialist Party to form the next government.

---

# Analysis

The election produced another balanced Parliament and further demonstrated the increasingly competitive nature of Agactan politics.

The Constitution Party made significant gains but remained short of being able to form a government. The Liberal Party once again held the balance of power and ultimately chose to enter coalition with the Socialist Party.

The Communist Party also made modest gains, while the Socialist Party suffered losses but remained the country's largest political force.

---

# Legacy

The 1977 election reinforced the Liberal Party's reputation as Agacta's principal kingmaker. It demonstrated that coalition negotiations, rather than simply the size of the largest party, often determined the composition of government.

The result also marked the first coalition government between the Socialist Party and Liberal Party, establishing a precedent that would be repeated in later decades.
