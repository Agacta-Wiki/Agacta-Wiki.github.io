# 2013 Agactan general election

The **2013 Agactan general election** was held on **___ 2013** to elect the 240 members of the [[Parliament of Agacta]].

The election resulted in a historic victory for the [[Constitution Party]], which emerged as the largest party in Parliament for the first time since the establishment of the modern republic. Following coalition negotiations, the Constitution Party and [[Liberal Party]] agreed to form a government, ending sixteen consecutive years of Socialist-led administrations.

The election also saw the [[Green Party]] record its strongest performance to date.

---

# Background

The election followed four years of Socialist-Green coalition government.

The governing coalition entered the campaign amid growing public concern over the economy, public finances and the aftermath of the global financial crisis. After sixteen years in government, the Socialist Party faced increasing voter fatigue, while the Constitution Party argued that Agacta required new political leadership and a fresh economic direction.

The Green Party sought to build upon its role in government, while the Agacta Left Bloc attempted to establish itself as a permanent force on the political left.

# Government formation

Following the election, the Constitution Party and Liberal Party agreed to form a coalition government with a combined total of **124 seats**.

The President subsequently invited the leader of the Constitution Party to form the next government, marking the first time the party had led the government as the largest party in Parliament.

---

# Analysis

The election represented one of the most significant political realignments in modern Agactan history.

For the first time since the adoption of the 1952 Constitution, the Constitution Party emerged as the country's largest political party, ending decades of Socialist electoral dominance.

The Socialist Party suffered one of the heaviest defeats in its history, losing twenty-seven seats after sixteen consecutive years in government.

The Liberal Party recovered from its disappointing 2009 result and once again entered government as the junior coalition partner.

The Green Party achieved its best election result to date, doubling its parliamentary representation and establishing itself as Agacta's fourth major political force.

Meanwhile, the Agacta Left Bloc experienced a modest decline but retained parliamentary representation.

---

# Legacy

The 2013 election is widely regarded as a watershed in Agactan political history.

It marked the beginning of the Constitution Party's longest continuous period in government and the first time it became the dominant force in Agactan politics.

The election also confirmed the Green Party as a major parliamentary party, while the Socialist Party entered a prolonged period of rebuilding following its worst defeat in decades.
