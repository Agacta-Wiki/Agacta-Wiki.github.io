# 2017 Agactan general election

The **2017 Agactan general election** was held on **___ 2017** to elect the 240 members of the [[Parliament of Agacta]].

The election resulted in a second consecutive victory for the [[Constitution Party]], which increased its representation and remained the largest party in Parliament. The [[Constitution Party]] and [[Liberal Party]] renewed their coalition government for a second term.

The election is chiefly remembered for the dramatic surge of the [[Green Party]], which came within four seats of overtaking the [[Socialist Party]] to become the Official Opposition. Despite suffering its worst electoral defeat in the history of the modern republic, the Socialists narrowly retained their position as the second-largest party in Parliament.

---

# Background

The election followed four years of Constitution Party-Liberal coalition government.

The Constitution Party campaigned on its record in government, arguing that economic growth and political stability should continue under its leadership. The Socialist Party, still recovering from its heavy defeat in 2013, sought to rebuild public confidence but struggled to establish itself as a credible alternative government.

The Green Party campaigned on environmental protection, sustainable economic development and political reform, attracting growing support among younger and urban voters.

# Government formation

Following the election, the Constitution Party and Liberal Party agreed to renew their coalition government, holding a combined total of **138 seats**.

The President subsequently invited the leader of the Constitution Party to form the next government.

---

# Analysis

The election produced one of the most dramatic shifts in modern Agactan political history.

The Constitution Party strengthened its position as the country's dominant political force, increasing its parliamentary representation for a second consecutive election.

The Socialist Party suffered its worst defeat since the establishment of the modern republic, losing thirty-eight seats. Nevertheless, it narrowly retained its position as the second-largest party in Parliament, finishing just four seats ahead of the rapidly rising Green Party.

The Green Party recorded the largest gains of any party, increasing its representation by twenty-seven seats and establishing itself as a major force in Agactan politics. Although it fell just short of becoming the Official Opposition, the result demonstrated that it had become a genuine challenger to the country's traditional political parties.

The Liberal Party also recorded significant gains and comfortably retained its position as the Constitution Party's preferred coalition partner.

Meanwhile, the Agacta Left Bloc experienced further decline, recording its weakest performance since entering Parliament in 2009.

---

# Legacy

The 2017 election marked a decisive shift in the balance of Agactan politics.

While the Constitution Party consolidated its position as the country's dominant governing party, the Green Party emerged as a major national political force and came within four seats of becoming the Official Opposition.

Although the Socialist Party narrowly remained the second-largest party, its dramatically reduced representation led many political commentators to question whether Agacta's traditional political order was beginning to give way to a new multi-party era.

The result also laid the foundations for the closely contested political landscape that would emerge at the following general election.
