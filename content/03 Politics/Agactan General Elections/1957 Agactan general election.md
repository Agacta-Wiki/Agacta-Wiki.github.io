The **1957 Agactan general election** was held on **___ 1957** to elect the 240 members of the [[Parliament of Agacta]]. It was the second general election held under the [[Constitution of Agacta|1952 Constitution]].  
  
The election resulted in a landslide victory for the governing [[Socialist Party]], led by Prime Minister [[Josio Valtro]], who secured an increased parliamentary majority and a second consecutive term in office.  
  
---  
  
# Background  
  
The election took place during the formative years of the modern republic. Since the first constitutional government, Prime Minister Josio Valtro had overseen the consolidation of the state's democratic institutions while promoting economic development, agricultural modernisation and national reconstruction.  
  
The campaign focused largely on economic management, housing, agriculture, defence and the long-term direction of the young republic.  
  
---  
  
# Campaign  
  
Prime Minister **Josio Valtro** campaigned on his government's record of stability and nation-building. A decorated general during the Alaramian invasion of Setroburg and one of the principal architects of the modern republic, Valtro advocated a broad, centrist vision for Agacta.  
  
His government promoted affordable housing programmes, subsidies for farmers, investment in infrastructure and a policy of national self-sufficiency while remaining supportive of international cooperation. Valtro also emphasised strong national defence, protection of the Agactan language and support for domestic industry and agriculture. His governments were also notable for opening Agacta's television industry to private broadcasters decades before similar liberalisation occurred across much of Europe.  
  
The [[Constitution Party]], led by **Elbert Kelmar**, campaigned on fiscal responsibility, balanced budgets and support for private enterprise. While presenting a clear alternative on economic management, Kelmar remained politically moderate and shared many of Valtro's patriotic and institutional priorities.  
  
The [[Liberal Party]], under **Jin Solten**, campaigned from the political centre, advocating constitutional liberalism, civil liberties and free enterprise while promoting itself as a pragmatic alternative to the two larger parties.  
  
The [[Communist Party]], led by the 27-year-old **Riki Rovmar**, argued for greater public ownership and workers' rights. Rovmar, later widely known by the nickname **"Ricko Rovmar,"** was viewed at the time as an energetic grassroots activist whose vision of communism was rooted in ideals of international peace and solidarity rather than geopolitical alignment. His later closeness to Ricko and Alaram would become increasingly controversial in subsequent decades.

# Government formation  
  
With 148 of the 240 seats in Parliament, the Socialist Party comfortably retained its parliamentary majority.  
  
President [[President of Agacta]] invited Josio Valtro to form the next government, becoming the first Prime Minister of the modern republic to win re-election.  
  
---  
  
# Analysis  
  
The election confirmed the Socialist Party's dominant position during the republic's early decades.  
  
Valtro's popularity as a wartime commander, founding father of the republic and principal architect of the 1952 Constitution proved decisive. His broad political appeal enabled the Socialist Party to attract support across both urban and rural Agacta.  
  
Although the Constitution Party remained the principal opposition, its programme was often viewed as differing more in emphasis than ideology from that of the government, reflecting the broad centrist consensus that characterised early republican politics.  
  
The Liberal Party remained a significant parliamentary presence despite losing seats, while the Communist Party continued to occupy the political fringe.  
  
---  
  
# Legacy  
  
Historians generally regard the 1957 election as the high-water mark of the early Valtro era.  
  
The election entrenched the Socialist Party as the natural party of government and cemented Josio Valtro's reputation as one of the principal founding figures of the modern Agactan state. Alongside his military leadership during the Alaramian invasion, his role in drafting the Constitution and helping establish the [[SADS]] alliance, Valtro is widely regarded as one of the most influential statesmen in Agactan history.
