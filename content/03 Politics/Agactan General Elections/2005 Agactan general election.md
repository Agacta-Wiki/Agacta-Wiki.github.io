# 2005 Agactan general election

The **2005 Agactan general election** was held on **___ 2005** to elect the 240 members of the [[Parliament of Agacta]].

The election resulted in the [[Socialist Party]] retaining its parliamentary majority for a third consecutive term. Although the party lost seats compared with its landslide victory in 2001, it remained comfortably the largest political force in Agacta.

The [[Constitution Party]] made significant gains, while the [[Green Party]] continued its gradual parliamentary growth.

---

# Background

The election followed four years of Socialist majority government.

The governing party campaigned on its record of economic growth, public investment and political stability. Meanwhile, the Constitution Party sought to rebuild following its historic defeat in 2001, presenting itself as a credible alternative government.

The Green Party also continued to campaign on environmental protection and sustainable development as those issues became increasingly prominent in public debate.

# Government formation

The Socialist Party retained an overall parliamentary majority with **137 seats**, allowing it to continue governing without coalition partners.

The President subsequently invited the leader of the Socialist Party to form the next government.

---

# Analysis

The election represented a modest setback for the governing Socialist Party following its landslide victory four years earlier. Nevertheless, it remained comfortably ahead of its rivals and secured another outright majority.

The Constitution Party recorded a strong recovery, gaining fifteen seats and reversing some of the heavy losses suffered in 2001. Although still well behind the Socialists, the result restored confidence within the party and re-established it as a credible challenger for government.

The Liberal Party lost ground but maintained a substantial parliamentary presence, while the Green Party continued its steady upward trajectory by reaching double-digit representation for the first time.

---

# Legacy

The 2005 election is generally regarded as the beginning of the Constitution Party's recovery after its historic defeat in 2001.

While the Socialist Party remained dominant, the result suggested that Agactan politics was becoming more competitive once again. The election also confirmed the Green Party as an established parliamentary force rather than a minor protest movement.
