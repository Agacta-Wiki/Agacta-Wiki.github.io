# Politics of Agacta

## Overview

Politics in Agacta operates within the framework of a parliamentary republic. Executive power is exercised by the Prime Minister and Cabinet, who are responsible to the 240-member Parliament of Agacta. The President serves as head of state in a largely ceremonial role.

Agacta elects its Parliament every four years using a mixed-member proportional (MMP) electoral system. Since the adoption of the modern constitution, governments have almost always been coalitions, with the Liberal Party frequently serving as kingmaker between the country's two largest parties.

---

# Political system

## Republic

Agacta is a parliamentary republic with a largely ceremonial President serving as head of state.

Executive authority is exercised by the Prime Minister, who commands the confidence of Parliament.

The President performs constitutional and ceremonial duties, including appointing governments, promulgating legislation, and representing Agacta on state occasions.

## Parliament

The Parliament of Agacta consists of **240 Members of Parliament (MPs)** elected every four years.

It is a unicameral legislature responsible for:

- Passing legislation
- Approving the national budget
- Holding the Government to account
- Electing and supporting the Prime Minister

## Electoral system

Agacta uses a mixed-member proportional (MMP) electoral system.

The country is divided into **19 constituencies**, each corresponding to one mainland province or overseas territory. Half of all MPs are elected from single-member constituencies, while the remainder are elected from regional party lists.

Unlike some other MMP systems, Agacta deliberately prioritises local representation over perfect proportionality. Constituency seats are protected and list seats are adjusted within each constituency to maintain broad proportionality while avoiding excessive overhang seats.

# Political parties

Since the late twentieth century Agactan politics has been dominated by five principal political parties.

## Constitution Party

The Constitution Party is a centre-right political party founded during Agacta's independence era in the nineteenth century.

Historically the country's second-largest party, it overtook the Socialist Party during the 2010s and has led the government continuously since 2013.

Although generally regarded as centre-right, its policies are often comparatively centrist by international standards.

## Socialist Party

The Socialist Party is a centre-left political party founded during the 1920s.

For much of modern Agactan history it was the country's dominant political force and supplied the majority of Prime Ministers.

While officially centre-left, it has often governed from the political centre. Its long-standing rivalry with the Constitution Party has often been compared to the relationship between Ireland's two traditionally dominant parties, although it is rooted in ideology rather than civil war.

Following heavy electoral defeats during the 2010s and early 2020s, the party entered a period of rebuilding after the 2025 general election.

## Liberal Party

The Liberal Party occupies the political centre.

Although rarely the largest party, it has frequently held the balance of power and has participated in numerous coalition governments with both major parties.

Because of its repeated role as junior coalition partner, it is jokingly referred to as **"the Party of the Deputy Prime Minister."**

Its preferred coalition partner has traditionally been the Constitution Party, although it has also governed alongside the Socialist Party when parliamentary arithmetic has favoured such an arrangement.

## Green Party

The Green Party emerged as Agacta's principal environmental party following the collapse of the Communist Party.

After entering Parliament for the first time in 1997, it steadily expanded its representation over the following two decades.

Its greatest success came in the 2021 general election, when it became the Official Opposition after overtaking both the Socialist and Liberal parties.

However, following an ineffective period in opposition and increased competition from the Agacta Left Bloc, the party suffered significant losses in the 2025 election.

## Agacta Left Bloc

The Agacta Left Bloc (AMB) is a democratic socialist alliance founded in 2009.

Initially a minor parliamentary force, the party experienced rapid growth following the COVID-19 pandemic and the subsequent inflation crisis.

In the 2025 general election it made substantial gains, attracting many former Green and Socialist voters and establishing itself as one of Agacta's major parliamentary parties.

# Historical development

## Four-party era

For much of the twentieth century Agacta was characterised by a stable four-party system consisting of the:

- Socialist Party
- Constitution Party
- Liberal Party
- Communist Party

The Socialists were generally the largest party, while the Constitution Party consistently ranked second.

The Liberals regularly held the balance of power, while the Communists maintained a small but consistent parliamentary presence.

## Collapse of the Communist Party

Following the political changes of 1989, the Communist Party rapidly declined and eventually disappeared from parliamentary politics.

Its place was gradually taken by the Green Party during the 1990s.

## Rise of the Green Party

The Green Party first entered Parliament in 1997.

Over the following two decades it steadily expanded its support, culminating in becoming the Official Opposition following the 2021 general election.

## Five-party era

The emergence of the Agacta Left Bloc transformed Agacta into a five-party political system.

As of the 2025 general election, parliamentary politics is dominated by the:

- Constitution Party
- Socialist Party
- Liberal Party
- Green Party
- Agacta Left Bloc

---

# Coalition politics

Coalition governments have been a common feature of Agactan politics.

The Liberal Party has frequently held the balance of power and has governed alongside both the Constitution Party and the Socialist Party.

Despite their relatively moderate ideological differences, a coalition between the Constitution Party and the Socialist Party has never occurred and remains politically taboo.

---

# Current political landscape

The 2025 general election returned the Constitution Party and Liberal Party to office for a historic fourth consecutive term.

The Socialist Party remains the principal opposition while continuing its recovery from earlier electoral setbacks.

Meanwhile, the Agacta Left Bloc has emerged as one of the country's fastest-growing political movements following a breakthrough election in 2025.
