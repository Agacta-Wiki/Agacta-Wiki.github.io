# Parliamentary elections in Agacta

Parliamentary elections have been held every four years in Agacta since the introduction of the 1952 Constitution. Members of Parliament are elected using the country's mixed-member proportional (MMP) electoral system.

Since the establishment of the modern republic, Agactan politics has been dominated by the Constitution Party, Socialist Party and Liberal Party, with the Green Party and Agacta Left Bloc becoming significant forces in the twenty-first century.

## Political eras

### Socialist dominance (1953–1973)

During the first two decades of the modern republic, the Socialist Party dominated Agactan politics and produced every Prime Minister.

### Competitive two-party era (1977–2009)

From the late 1970s onward, elections became increasingly competitive between the Socialist Party and Constitution Party, with the Liberal Party frequently holding the balance of power.

### Constitution era (2013–present)

Since 2013 the Constitution Party has led four consecutive governments in coalition with the Liberal Party, marking the longest continuous period of Constitution-led government in Agactan history.
