# SLC Creative Team

## Overview

The **Super League Combat (SLC)** creative team is responsible
for the promotion's long-term storylines, match planning,
character development, roster direction, and overall
television presentation.

The core creative team consists of:

- **Jim Barry** — SLC President and Head Booker
- **Lemonbeck** — Creative Team
- **Limebeck** — Creative Team
- **Kiana Martins** — Creative Team
- **Tropicbeck** — Creative Team

Jim Barry leads the booking process and has final creative
authority, while Lemonbeck, Limebeck, Kiana Martins, and
Tropicbeck contribute ideas and help develop the promotion
under his direction.

The group deliberately contains several different wrestling
philosophies.

Rather than attempting to make everyone agree on what
professional wrestling should be, SLC uses those differences
to create a broader product.

The central creative principle is that SLC should combine:

- long-term storytelling;
- believable wrestling;
- strong characters;
- unpredictability;
- stylistic variety;
- an underground atmosphere;
- enough structure that stories remain coherent.

The creative team plans major storylines well in advance
but allows wrestlers considerable freedom to improvise
dialogue, develop mannerisms, and contribute ideas to
their own characters.

# Creative Leadership

## Jim Barry

**Role:** SLC President / Head Booker

Jim Barry leads SLC's creative direction and retains
responsibility for both long-term booking and the overall
shape of weekly television.

Although his responsibilities as SLC president and his wider
work with **Invictus Sport** keep him busy, he remains the
head booker.

He works collaboratively with Lemonbeck, Limebeck, Kiana
Martins, and Tropicbeck, drawing on their different strengths
rather than delegating final booking authority to any one
of them.

Barry chairs creative discussions, decides the final direction
when ideas conflict, and ensures that individual storylines
serve the promotion as a whole.

## Lemonbeck

**Role:** Creative Team / Television Storytelling and Presentation

Lemonbeck is a leading contributor to SLC's television
storytelling, character presentation, and longer-running
dramatic angles.

Jim Barry remains head booker and oversees the creative
direction of SLC.

Lemonbeck pitches ideas, develops stories with the rest of
the team, and helps shape the weekly television product,
but does not control booking decisions.

His influence helps make SLC colourful and unpredictable
without displacing Barry's emphasis on balance and coherence.

# Creative Meetings

SLC's creative meetings are collaborative.

Jim Barry chairs the meetings as head booker, but major
ideas are openly debated.

Every member is encouraged to challenge a proposal,
suggest alternatives, and contribute from their own
perspective.

A typical discussion may involve:

- Lemonbeck asking what creates the best television story;
- Limebeck asking whether the competitive logic makes sense;
- Kiana asking what the result changes for each wrestler;
- Tropicbeck asking whether the characters remain consistent;
- Jim Barry weighing those perspectives, considering the
  flow of the whole show, and deciding what best serves SLC.

The goal is not consensus on every detail, and the creative
team does not vote to overrule the head booker.

The different perspectives are considered one of SLC's
strengths.

Barry encourages creative disagreements because he believes
the strongest ideas frequently emerge when different
wrestling philosophies are brought together.

However, the final responsibility for SLC's direction
rests with him.
