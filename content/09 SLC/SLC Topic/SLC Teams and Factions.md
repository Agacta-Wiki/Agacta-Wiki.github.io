# SLC Teams and Factions

## Overview

Teams and factions play an important role in **Super League Combat (SLC)**, but the promotion generally avoids creating groups simply to give wrestlers something to do.

Most teams are built around a clear shared identity, history, philosophy, or practical reason for existing.

Factions can influence championship races, backstage politics, rivalries, and alliances, while established tag teams are treated as specialist units rather than temporary combinations of singles wrestlers.

SLC currently features several major groups ranging from elite wrestling factions to veteran teams, street-based units, political alliances, and deliberately mismatched partnerships.

---

# The Industry Standard

**The Industry Standard** is one of SLC's most powerful factions.

### Members

- **Matt Riddle**
- **Simon Vale**
- **Yoon Tae-Seok**
- **Kevin Nash**

The group is based around the belief that its members represent an elite standard of professional wrestling and combat ability.

Rather than portraying themselves as traditional rule-breaking villains, members of The Industry Standard frequently attempt to prove their superiority through clean victories.

They view themselves as wrestlers who should define what excellence in SLC looks like.

## Matt Riddle

Matt Riddle is the faction's central figure and the first **SLC World Champion**.

Riddle presents himself as an elite combat athlete whose background and ability place him above most of the roster.

His personality combines confidence, arrogance, and elements of his more relaxed earlier persona.

## Simon Vale

Simon Vale represents technical and amateur wrestling within the faction.

Known as **The Technician**, Vale places enormous value on wrestling ability and considers technical excellence the purest measure of a competitor.

Although initially loyal to Riddle, his ambition and confidence have the potential to create questions over who should ultimately lead The Industry Standard.

## Yoon Tae-Seok

Yoon Tae-Seok represents a hard-hitting strong-style approach.

His disciplined striking and physical wrestling style give The Industry Standard a different dimension from Riddle and Vale.

His victory over **Dante Navarro** at Mayhem significantly increased his standing within SLC.

## Kevin Nash

Kevin Nash adds veteran experience, political intelligence, and star presence.

Unlike the younger members, Nash is not primarily presented as representing a particular wrestling style.

Instead, he functions as an experienced strategist who understands the wrestling business and how to influence it.

### Entrance Theme

The Industry Standard enters together to:

**"Spitfire" — The Prodigy**

# The Delegation

**The Delegation** is a faction led by **Quo Zabay**.

### Members and Associates

- **Quo Zabay**
- **Clark Montrose**
- **Eliot Voss**
- **Giovanni Vinci**
- **Mark Henry**

The Delegation is built around influence rather than a single wrestling philosophy.

Its members use political connections, negotiation, favours, opportunity, and strategic alliances to advance their interests.

The group is less concerned with proving itself as the best wrestling faction than with ensuring its members occupy advantageous positions.

## Quo Zabay

Quo Zabay, known as **The Diplomat**, is the faction's leader.

He presents himself as a sophisticated political operator who prefers to influence events rather than personally fight every battle.

Zabay is capable of competing at a high level but frequently sends other members of The Delegation to represent his interests.

## Champagne Syndicate

**Champagne Syndicate** consists of:

- **Clark Montrose**
- **Eliot Voss**

They are a polished, arrogant tag team and became the first **SLC Tag Team Champions** at Ignition.

Their refined image fits naturally with Zabay's emphasis on status, influence, and presentation.

## Giovanni Vinci

Giovanni Vinci provides The Delegation with a polished European singles wrestler capable of representing the group in important matches.

He often acts as one of Zabay's primary in-ring representatives.

## Mark Henry

Mark Henry serves as the faction's heavyweight presence.

His experience and physical size make him an effective enforcer when Zabay prefers to remain outside direct conflict.

# Pavement Cartel

**Pavement Cartel** is one of SLC's most established groups.

### Members

- **Behemoth**
- **Trayvon Steel**
- **Dee Woodland**

The group combines a street-level identity with three very different wrestling styles.

They are generally presented as a close-knit unit rather than a faction built around one dominant leader.

## Behemoth

Behemoth is the group's heavyweight powerhouse.

He can compete as part of the unit or independently in singles competition.

## Trayvon Steel

Trayvon Steel brings speed, athleticism, and cruiserweight-style offence.

He is considered the member with the greatest potential for a significant future singles run.

## Dee Woodland

Dee Woodland is primarily a tag-team specialist and provides stability to the unit.

### Identity

Pavement Cartel's presentation emphasizes loyalty, toughness, and street credibility.

They frequently operate as babyfaces or tweeners depending on their opponents.

# The Vertex

**The Vertex** is the veteran babyface team of:

- **Justin Gabriel**
- **Heath Slater**

The name revives the identity of an earlier celebrated wrestling group associated with Gabriel and Slater.

In SLC, however, The Vertex is not presented as a dominant faction attempting to take over the company.

Instead, it functions primarily as a respected veteran team whose history gives them significant credibility.

The Vertex is treated similarly to legendary tag teams who remain active while carrying the weight of their past achievements.

Their position generally remains around the upper mid-card and tag-team championship scene, although Gabriel is also capable of entering the World Championship picture.

### Catchphrase

> "You are either Vertex or against us."

### Entrance Theme

**"We Are One" — 12 Stones**

The return of the theme is presented as a nostalgia-driven moment rather than an attempt to recreate the past.

### Ryback

**Ryback** has history with Gabriel and Slater and may occasionally be associated with The Vertex.

He is not currently considered a permanent core member of the team.

# Rock and Rap Connection

**Rock and Rap Connection** consists of:

- **Alfie Rowe**
- **T-Style**

They are managed by:

- **Music Biz Bob**

The team developed from the collision of two very different musical personalities.

## Alfie Rowe

Alfie Rowe is heavily influenced by Britpop and carries himself with exaggerated rock-star swagger.

He initially considered hip-hop fundamentally incompatible with his own identity.

## T-Style

T-Style embraces hip-hop culture and speaks in exaggerated rap slang.

He originally attempted to join Pavement Cartel before being told that the group did not need another member.

## Formation

Music Biz Bob recognised the entertainment value in pairing Alfie and T-Style despite neither man initially wanting to team with the other.

Their contrasting personalities became the foundation of the act.

### Entrance Theme

**"Feel Good Inc." — Gorillaz**

The team later adopted a more antagonistic attitude, allowing their musical arrogance and personality clashes with other wrestlers to become more pronounced.

# Champagne Syndicate

**Champagne Syndicate** consists of:

- **Clark Montrose**
- **Eliot Voss**

Although part of The Delegation, Champagne Syndicate also operates independently as one of SLC's primary tag teams.

Their presentation is based around wealth, refinement, arrogance, and old-school tag-team coordination.

They became the first **SLC Tag Team Champions** by defeating Pavement Cartel at Ignition.

Their association with Quo Zabay gives them access to The Delegation's wider political influence.

---

# Bannons in Pyjamas

**Bannons in Pyjamas** consists of:

- **Jimmy Bannon**
- **Joe Bannon**

The team is primarily used as recurring comedy and enhancement talent.

Their presentation includes banana-inspired costumes and an intentionally absurd name, but they are generally portrayed as having genuine fighting spirit once matches begin.

The Bannons can appear sporadically without requiring continuous storylines.

### Entrance Theme

The team uses the **Bananas in Pyjamas** theme.

# Temporary and Informal Alliances

SLC also regularly features temporary alliances without automatically treating them as permanent teams.

These partnerships generally develop from storyline circumstances rather than from a desire to create additional formal factions.

Examples can include:

- wrestlers temporarily joining forces against a common enemy;
- veterans mentoring younger wrestlers;
- established singles wrestlers teaming during faction conflicts;
- one-off combinations created for tournaments or major events.

A temporary partnership is not automatically given a team name or treated as a long-term act.

---

# Team and Faction Philosophy

SLC attempts to keep its number of teams and factions manageable.

A stable should normally have a clear reason for existing beyond simply grouping wrestlers together.

Different groups serve different purposes.

**The Industry Standard** is built around wrestling superiority.

**The Delegation** is built around influence and political access.

**Pavement Cartel** is built around loyalty and shared identity.

**The Vertex** is built around history and veteran credibility.

**Rock and Rap Connection** is built around personality contrast.

**Champagne Syndicate** is built around a defined tag-team identity while also functioning inside a larger faction.

This approach is intended to ensure that teams remain memorable and that individual wrestlers retain distinct personalities even when they belong to a larger group.
