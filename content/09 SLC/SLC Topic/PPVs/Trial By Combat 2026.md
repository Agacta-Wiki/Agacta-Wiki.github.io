# Trial by Combat (2026)

**Trial by Combat (2026)** was a professional wrestling
pay-per-view event produced by **Super League Combat (SLC)**.
It took place on **25 September 2026** at the **SLC Arena**
in **Treslio, Agacta**.

The event was SLC's third pay-per-view, following *Ignition*
and *Mayhem*, and formed part of the promotion's
**Summer of Violence**.

Seven matches were contested, including three championship
matches.

In the main event, **Matt Riddle** defeated **Demolition Jones**
to retain the **SLC World Championship** after **24 minutes
and 18 seconds**, within the match's 30-minute time limit.

**Velociraptor** retained the **SLC Challenge Championship**
against **Simon Vale**, while **Champagne Syndicate** and
**The Vertex** wrestled to a **20-minute time-limit draw**,
allowing Champagne Syndicate to retain the
**SLC Tag Team Championship**.

Elsewhere, **Sheamus O'Shaughnessy** defeated **Yoon Tae-Seok**,
**Kevin Nash** defeated **George Pembrook**, **Dante Navarro
and Rob Van Dam** defeated **Mark Henry and Giovanni Vinci**,
and **Pavement Cartel** defeated **Desmond Boyce and Rock and
Rap Connection**.

## Background

Trial by Combat was presented as one of SLC's principal
competitive events.

Although it took place during the Summer of Violence,
the show was built predominantly around standard wrestling
matches rather than compulsory hardcore stipulations.

The seven-match card brought together several rivalries
that had developed since *Ignition*.

### SLC World Championship

The main event featured **Matt Riddle**, SLC's inaugural
World Champion and a leading figure in **Industry Standard**,
against **Demolition Jones**.

Jones had lost to Industry Standard member **Simon Vale**
at *Ignition* before defeating him in a rematch at *Mayhem*.

His rise into championship contention followed his victory
over **George Pembrook** in a number-one-contender match
on *Boom*.

Pembrook had entered that match at a disadvantage after
being attacked by **Kevin Nash**.

The championship rivalry emphasised the contrast between
Riddle's disciplined grappling and Jones's less predictable,
physically aggressive style.

Jones sought to prove that he could defeat Riddle in a
conventional wrestling contest rather than rely on disorder
or weapons.

Their title match was assigned a **30-minute time limit**.

### SLC Challenge Championship

**Velociraptor**, the inaugural Challenge Champion,
defended against **Simon Vale**.

Vale had maintained his standing as one of SLC's strongest
technical wrestlers despite his loss to Demolition Jones
at *Mayhem*.

The rivalry intensified after Vale defeated Velociraptor's
friend **Douglas Wright** and continued to apply an armbar
after the match.

A confrontation involving Industry Standard followed,
with Velociraptor and Jones coming to Wright's aid.

The championship match represented Vale's opportunity
to turn his technical credentials into a title victory
and Velociraptor's opportunity to defend both his
championship and his approach to wrestling.

### SLC Tag Team Championship

The reigning **SLC Tag Team Champions**,
**Champagne Syndicate** (**Clark Montrose** and **Eliot Voss**),
defended against **The Vertex** (**Justin Gabriel** and
**Heath Slater**).

Champagne Syndicate had won the titles at *Ignition*
and were aligned with **Quo Zabay's Delegation**.

Gabriel and Slater, meanwhile, had reaffirmed their
partnership during the build to *Mayhem*, when Slater
prevented Nash from interfering in Gabriel's World
Championship match against Riddle.

The meeting offered a contrast between Champagne
Syndicate's established tag-team coordination and the
experience and shared history of The Vertex.

### Other rivalries

**Dante Navarro** entered the event looking to recover
from his defeat by **Yoon Tae-Seok** at *Mayhem*.

**Rob Van Dam**, who had begun mentoring Navarro,
joined him against Delegation representatives
**Mark Henry** and **Giovanni Vinci**, turning Navarro's
attempt to rebuild confidence into a direct confrontation
with Zabay's group.

**Sheamus O'Shaughnessy**, a recent addition to the
SLC roster, faced Yoon Tae-Seok in a contest between
two hard-hitting competitors.

Sheamus had previously intervened to prevent Yoon
from taking advantage of a distracted referee, giving
their rivalry a more personal dimension.

**Kevin Nash** faced **George Pembrook** following Nash's
attempt to manipulate the World Championship contender
process and his subsequent attack on Pembrook.

Nash's frustration at being excluded from the contender
match gave the rivalry its initial direction.

The opening six-man tag team match brought together
**Pavement Cartel** (**Behemoth**, **Trayvon Steel** and
**Dee Woodland**) against **Desmond Boyce** and
**Rock and Rap Connection** (**Alfie Rowe** and **T-Style**).

The match followed Pavement Cartel's victory over Rock
and Rap Connection and Boyce's singles victory over
Behemoth at *Mayhem*.

## Event

### Pavement Cartel vs. Desmond Boyce and Rock and Rap Connection

The opening match was a six-man tag team contest between
**Pavement Cartel** and the combination of **Desmond Boyce**,
**Alfie Rowe** and **T-Style**.

The teams entered with separate but overlapping histories
from *Mayhem*: Pavement Cartel had defeated Rock and Rap
Connection, while Boyce had beaten Behemoth in singles
competition.

The match therefore gave all six wrestlers an opportunity
to settle unfinished business within a team setting.

**Pavement Cartel won after 11 minutes and 28 seconds.**

The result gave the group another pay-per-view victory
over Rock and Rap Connection and restored momentum to
the Cartel side of the Boyce–Behemoth rivalry.

### Dante Navarro and Rob Van Dam vs. Mark Henry and Giovanni Vinci

**Dante Navarro** teamed with his mentor **Rob Van Dam**
against **Mark Henry** and **Giovanni Vinci**, representing
**The Delegation**.

The pairing was significant for Navarro, whose promising
start in SLC had been interrupted by a defeat to Yoon
Tae-Seok at *Mayhem*.

Van Dam's involvement reflected his growing role in
helping the younger wrestler regain confidence, while
Henry and Vinci provided physically imposing and
experienced opposition.

**Navarro and Van Dam won after 9 minutes and 23 seconds.**

It was Navarro's first pay-per-view victory since
*Ignition* and an important positive result in his
recovery from the Mayhem setback.

### Kevin Nash vs. George Pembrook

**Kevin Nash** faced **George Pembrook** in the third match.

The contest developed from Nash's anger at being left
out of the World Championship contender process.

After failing to secure a place in the relevant match
through the SLC championship committee, Nash attacked
Pembrook ahead of his meeting with Demolition Jones,
adding a personal grievance to the sporting rivalry.

Pembrook had previously scored an upset victory over
Mark Henry at *Mayhem*, but faced another experienced
and considerably larger opponent in Nash.

**Nash won after 8 minutes and 26 seconds.**

The victory strengthened Nash's position after his
earlier pay-per-view loss to Dante Navarro, although
the dispute arising from his attack on Pembrook remained
an important part of the rivalry's history.

### SLC Tag Team Championship

**Champagne Syndicate (c)** defended the **SLC Tag Team
Championship** against **The Vertex**, consisting of
Justin Gabriel and Heath Slater.

The match represented a major opportunity for Gabriel
and Slater to challenge the inaugural champions as an
established partnership.

For Clark Montrose and Eliot Voss, it was a test against
a veteran team with considerable collective experience.

**The match ended in a draw when the 20-minute time
limit expired.**

Neither team secured a decisive victory within the
allotted time, and **Champagne Syndicate retained the
SLC Tag Team Championship**.

The result was the night's only draw and its only
championship contest without a winner.

It preserved both teams' standing in the tag division
while leaving their direct competitive comparison
unresolved.

### Sheamus O'Shaughnessy vs. Yoon Tae-Seok

**Sheamus O'Shaughnessy** faced **Yoon Tae-Seok**
in the fifth match.

Yoon had become an increasingly significant member
of Industry Standard after defeating Dante Navarro
at *Mayhem*.

Sheamus, by contrast, had arrived in SLC as an
internationally established wrestler and made an
immediate impression through his willingness to
confront Yoon's conduct.

The match paired two wrestlers associated with physical,
striking-heavy styles and gave Sheamus an early major-event
test against one of SLC's ascending competitors.

**Sheamus defeated Yoon after 15 minutes and 22 seconds.**

The victory strengthened Sheamus's position in SLC
while bringing Yoon's run of pay-per-view momentum
to a halt.

### SLC Challenge Championship

**Velociraptor (c)** defended the **SLC Challenge
Championship** against **Simon Vale**.

The match followed Vale's victory over Douglas Wright
and his subsequent attack after the bell, which had
brought Velociraptor directly into conflict with
Industry Standard.

It also gave Vale the chance to win a championship
after splitting his first two pay-per-view meetings
with Demolition Jones.

The contest contrasted Velociraptor's athletic,
adaptable approach with Vale's emphasis on technique,
control and submissions.

**Velociraptor defeated Vale after 18 minutes and
56 seconds**, successfully retaining the
**SLC Challenge Championship**.

The result continued Velociraptor's reign and denied
Industry Standard a second singles championship.

### SLC World Championship

In the main event, **Matt Riddle (c)** defended the
**SLC World Championship** against **Demolition Jones**
in a match with a **30-minute time limit**.

Riddle had become the inaugural champion by defeating
Rob Van Dam at *Ignition* and had retained against
Justin Gabriel at *Mayhem*.

Jones entered as number-one contender, having established
that he could compete successfully with Industry Standard
wrestlers in conventional matches.

The match pitted Riddle's technical and submission-oriented
offence against Jones's power, aggression and unpredictable
style.

With the time limit providing a clear competitive boundary,
Jones had to defeat Riddle rather than simply outlast him.

**Riddle defeated Jones after 24 minutes and 18 seconds**,
retaining the **SLC World Championship** with
**5 minutes and 42 seconds remaining** before the time limit.

At 24:18, it was the longest match on the card.

The result marked Riddle's **second successful pay-per-view
defence** of the championship and extended his unbeaten
record in SLC pay-per-view main events.

## Reception

Trial by Combat's results offered several talking points
for SLC's in-universe wrestling press.

The main event reinforced Matt Riddle's position as World
Champion, while Velociraptor's victory over Simon Vale
preserved the Challenge Championship's competitive importance.

The tag-title time-limit draw was the card's most
conspicuously unresolved result, contrasting with SLC's
usual preference for decisive finishes.

The show also balanced its title matches with several
shorter contests.

In particular, the Navarro–Van Dam tag match and
Nash–Pembrook singles match both ended in under ten
minutes, avoiding a run of lengthy matches throughout
the card.

## Aftermath

**Matt Riddle** remained SLC World Champion following
his second successful pay-per-view title defence.

The result strengthened his position at the top of the
promotion and denied Demolition Jones his first SLC
singles championship.

**Velociraptor** continued his reign as Challenge Champion
after defeating Simon Vale.

Vale's failure to capture the title also gave Industry
Standard a second significant defeat on the night,
following Yoon Tae-Seok's loss to Sheamus.

The **20-minute draw** between **Champagne Syndicate**
and **The Vertex** left the SLC Tag Team Championship
unchanged but their rivalry without a definitive winner.

Any future championship challenge would have to account
for the fact that neither team defeated the other at
Trial by Combat.

**Dante Navarro** gained an important victory alongside
**Rob Van Dam**.

The result supported the developing mentor–student
partnership and gave Navarro renewed momentum after
his defeat at *Mayhem*.

**Kevin Nash** defeated George Pembrook, while
**Sheamus O'Shaughnessy** secured a significant win
over Yoon Tae-Seok.

**Pavement Cartel** also strengthened its standing
against Boyce and Rock and Rap Connection.

With all three championships staying with their existing
holders, Trial by Combat was defined less by title
changes than by the consolidation of established champions
and the changing fortunes of the challengers and factions
beneath them.
