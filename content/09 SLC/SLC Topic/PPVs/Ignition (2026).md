# Ignition (2026)

**Ignition (2026)** was a professional wrestling pay-per-view event produced by **Super League Combat (SLC)**. It took place on **17 April 2026** at the **SLC Arena** in Treslio, Agacta.

The event was the first pay-per-view in SLC history and was held in front of a sell-out crowd of **1,500 spectators**.

Six matches were contested at the event. Three inaugural championships were decided, with **Matt Riddle** defeating **Rob Van Dam** to become the first SLC World Champion, **Velociraptor** defeating **Desmond Boyce** to become the inaugural SLC Challenge Champion, and **Champagne Syndicate** defeating **Pavement Cartel** to become the first SLC Tag Team Champions.

Elsewhere, **Dante Navarro** defeated veteran **Kevin Nash**, **Simon Vale** defeated **Demolition Jones**, and **Quo Zabay** defeated **George Pembrook**.

## Background

Ignition served as the formal launch of SLC's pay-per-view calendar and established several of the promotion's major competitive and character directions.

Three championships were introduced at the event:

- the **SLC World Championship**;
- the **SLC Challenge Championship**;
- the **SLC Tag Team Championship**.

The main event featured **Matt Riddle** against **Rob Van Dam** for the inaugural SLC World Championship.

Riddle entered the event as one of SLC's leading heels, presenting himself as an elite combat athlete and arguing that his background made him the natural standard-bearer for the new promotion.

Van Dam represented a contrasting philosophy, combining veteran credibility with the unconventional style and independent-wrestling influence that SLC intended to embrace.

The inaugural Challenge Championship was contested between **Velociraptor** and **Desmond Boyce**.

The match contrasted Velociraptor's athletic, independent-wrestling style with Boyce's theatrical and self-promotional personality.

The inaugural Tag Team Championship match featured **Champagne Syndicate**, consisting of **Clark Montrose and Eliot Voss**, against **Pavement Cartel**.

Other major matches included rising SLC prospect **Dante Navarro** facing former world champion **Kevin Nash**, while technical specialist **Simon Vale** faced the unpredictable **Demolition Jones**.

---

## Event

### Quo Zabay vs. George Pembrook

The opening match featured **Quo Zabay** against **George Pembrook**.

Pembrook used his speed and high-flying offence to frustrate Zabay, while Zabay attempted to slow the pace and control the contest.

Zabay ultimately won after **15 minutes and 32 seconds**.

The match established both characters on SLC's first major event, with Pembrook's eccentric presentation attracting attention despite the defeat.

### SLC Tag Team Championship

The second match saw **Champagne Syndicate** face **Pavement Cartel** to determine the inaugural SLC Tag Team Champions.

Champagne Syndicate used their more structured and traditional tag-team style against the contrasting power and athleticism of Pavement Cartel.

After **17 minutes and 7 seconds**, Champagne Syndicate won the match and became the first **SLC Tag Team Champions**.

### Simon Vale vs. Demolition Jones

**Simon Vale** faced **Demolition Jones** in the third match.

The contest was built around a clash of philosophies, with Vale attempting to keep the match technical and controlled while Jones repeatedly attempted to increase the physicality.

Vale eventually defeated Jones after **23 minutes and 28 seconds**.

The result established Vale as one of SLC's leading technical wrestlers while Jones remained competitive despite wrestling outside his preferred chaotic environment.

### Dante Navarro vs. Kevin Nash

The fourth match saw **Dante Navarro** face veteran **Kevin Nash**.

Unlike several other matches on the card, the contest was relatively short.

Navarro relied on speed and aerial offence to avoid prolonged exchanges with Nash, while Nash attempted to overpower the younger wrestler.

Navarro scored the victory after **10 minutes and 43 seconds**, giving him one of the most significant wins of his early SLC career.

The result was widely viewed as an early indication that SLC intended to use established veterans to elevate its younger roster rather than automatically positioning veterans above homegrown talent.

### SLC Challenge Championship

The penultimate match saw **Velociraptor** face **Desmond Boyce** for the inaugural **SLC Challenge Championship**.

The match lasted **24 minutes and 48 seconds** and was one of the longest contests of the event.

Velociraptor ultimately defeated Boyce to become the first Challenge Champion.

The match helped establish the Challenge Championship as a significant competitive title rather than a conventional lower-card championship.

### SLC World Championship

The main event featured **Matt Riddle** against **Rob Van Dam** for the inaugural **SLC World Championship**.

At **28 minutes and 56 seconds**, it was the longest match on the card.

The match combined Riddle's grappling and striking with Van Dam's unconventional offence and veteran experience.

Riddle ultimately defeated Van Dam to become the first **SLC World Champion**.

The victory established Riddle as SLC's first leading champion and immediately positioned him as one of the central figures of the promotion.

## Reception

Ignition received generally positive reviews, with critics particularly praising the event's clear match structure, relatively small card, and willingness to place younger SLC talent alongside established veterans.

Some criticism was directed at the length of several matches, with three contests exceeding 23 minutes on a six-match card.

### Critical scores

The following scores represent fictional in-universe wrestling criticism used within the SLC setting.

| Reviewer | Rating | Summary |
|---|---:|---|
| **Agacta Wrestling Journal** | **8.4/10** | Praised the clear identity of the promotion and the decision to establish younger talent without diminishing the veterans. |
| **RingScope** | **8/10** | Considered the main event and Challenge Championship match the strongest bouts but felt Vale vs. Jones ran slightly longer than necessary. |
| **Continental Wrestling Review** | **4/5** | Praised the six-match format and clean presentation, calling Navarro's victory over Nash the most important booking decision of the night. |

### Match ratings

| Velociraptor vs. Desmond Boyce | ★★★★ |

The Riddle–Van Dam main event was generally regarded as the best match of the event, with praise given to its contrast of styles and its importance in establishing the first SLC World Champion.

Velociraptor against Boyce also received strong reviews, particularly for establishing the Challenge Championship as a meaningful title from its first match.

Navarro's victory over Nash received less praise for match quality than the two title matches but was frequently cited as one of the most important moments of the show because of its implications for Navarro's future.

Criticism of the event largely concerned pacing. With the Vale–Jones, Velociraptor–Boyce, and Riddle–Van Dam matches all approaching or exceeding 25 minutes, some reviewers argued that the card could have benefited from a shorter match between the longer contests.

---

## Aftermath

Matt Riddle's victory established him as the inaugural SLC World Champion and placed him at the centre of SLC's first major championship programmes.

Velociraptor began his reign as the inaugural Challenge Champion, while Champagne Syndicate entered SLC's developing tag-team division as its first champions.

Dante Navarro's victory over Kevin Nash significantly raised his standing within SLC and reinforced his position as one of the promotion's leading younger wrestlers.

Simon Vale's victory over Demolition Jones strengthened his position within the upper midcard, while Jones remained a prominent part of SLC programming despite the loss.

Several of the rivalries and character developments established at Ignition continued into **Mayhem**, SLC's next pay-per-view event.
