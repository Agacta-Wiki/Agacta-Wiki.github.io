# Super League Combat (SLC)

## Promotion Overview

### Identity

**Super League Combat (SLC)** is a professional wrestling promotion built around three fundamental principles:

1. **Sports-based professional wrestling**
    
2. **High-quality matches featuring a variety of wrestling styles**
    
3. **Compelling, internally consistent storytelling**
    

SLC combines the competitive atmosphere of a sporting organisation with the characters, rivalries and ongoing narratives of professional wrestling.

The promotion operates from a converted, abandoned airport, giving its events a distinctive underground atmosphere.

SLC is not attempting to recreate WWE, AEW, ECW or any other wrestling organisation. Its ambition is to develop its own identity by combining elements of different wrestling traditions.

### In-Ring Philosophy

The quality of the wrestling is central to SLC's identity.

The promotion encourages a broad range of styles, including:

- Technical and submission wrestling
    
- High-flying and cruiserweight wrestling
    
- Strong style
    
- Lucha libre
    
- MMA-influenced wrestling
    
- Powerhouse wrestling
    
- Traditional brawling
    
- Hardcore wrestling, when appropriate
    

No single style dominates the promotion.

Different styles are encouraged to coexist, and matches between contrasting wrestlers are an important part of SLC's appeal.

**Clean finishes are strongly preferred.**

SLC wants victories and defeats to mean something. A wrestler should be capable of winning because they are better than their opponent on a particular night.

Heels do not automatically need to cheat to win, and babyfaces do not need to be protected from every defeat.

Interference, disqualifications and other controversial finishes remain possible, but they should serve specific storytelling purposes rather than become routine booking devices.

### Match Quality and Variety

SLC aims to produce consistently entertaining wrestling without expecting every match to become an epic.

Match lengths, pacing and styles should vary throughout a show.

A short, decisive victory can be just as valuable as a competitive twenty-minute contest when it serves the appropriate purpose.

The promotion particularly values:

- Believable competition
    
- Logical match psychology
    
- Good pacing
    
- Distinctive wrestling styles
    
- Strong finishes
    
- Matches that advance characters or competitive standings
    

Excessively choreographed sequences that undermine the illusion of competition are discouraged.

Major matches should feel important, and unusually long matches should remain special.

### Championships and Competition

SLC treats its championships as genuine sporting achievements within its fictional universe.

The promotion's principal championships are:

- **SLC World Championship**
    
- **SLC Tag Team Championship**
    
- **SLC Television Championship**
    

Championship opportunities should normally emerge from competitive accomplishments, established rivalries or clearly explained circumstances.
