## Super League Combat

**Super League Combat (SLC)** is a professional wrestling promotion based in **Treslio, Agacta**. It operates from the **SLC Arena**, a former airport complex converted into a wrestling venue, training base, production facility, and company headquarters.

SLC presents itself as a major alternative within professional wrestling rather than as a direct competitor to WWE, AEW, TNA, or other promotions. Its identity combines an intimate underground atmosphere, long-term storytelling, a relatively compact roster, and a broad mix of wrestling styles including technical wrestling, strong style, high flying, hardcore wrestling, and more traditional heavyweight wrestling.

The promotion is known for its rough-edged presentation and close relationship with its audience. The converted airport venue is central to SLC's image, giving shows a distinctive industrial feel and helping create the sense that fans have discovered a cult promotion operating slightly outside the mainstream wrestling world.

SLC places significant emphasis on **long-term narrative planning**. Storylines are generally mapped out in advance, while wrestlers are given freedom to improvise and develop promos within those broader plans. The promotion aims to balance wrestling and narrative rather than treating one as secondary to the other.

Its in-ring philosophy emphasizes believable competition without restricting wrestlers to a single house style. Heel wrestlers are not expected to cheat in every match, and clean victories by villains are common. Matches can end through finishers, ordinary high-impact moves, submissions, roll-ups, or other credible finishes, rather than requiring a finishing move in every case. Excessive false finishes and extremely long matches are relatively uncommon.

SLC also maintains a comparatively small active roster so that most regular performers remain familiar to the audience and have an identifiable role, direction, or storyline. The roster combines younger wrestlers developed or elevated within SLC, established industry veterans, and wrestlers whose careers are being repositioned or revived.

The promotion is owned by **Invictus Sport**, with **Jim Barry** serving as president. Day-to-day creative direction is led by **Lemonbeck**, with **Limebeck, Kiana Martins, and Tropicbeck** forming the remainder of the core creative team.

SLC's long-term ambition is to establish itself as the **third major promotion in professional wrestling**, while maintaining the smaller-scale, cult atmosphere that defines its identity.
