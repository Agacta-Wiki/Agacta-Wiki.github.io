## Provisional Card

### SLC World Championship
**Matt Riddle (c) vs Sheamus O'Shaughnessy**

- First World Championship meeting between Riddle and Sheamus.
- Sheamus enters the programme after his victory over Yoon Tae-Seok.
- Current plan: Riddle retains.
- The finish should leave enough material for a possible second match without undermining SLC's sporting logic.

---

### Dante Navarro vs Simon Vale

- No elaborate stipulation or interference.
- The intention is simply to let both wrestlers go out and have an exceptional match.
- Target is a long match of approximately 35 minutes.
- Intended to become one of the defining early matches in SLC history.
- **Current plan: Simon Vale wins clean.**
- This plants a long-term Navarro/Vale rivalry without immediately booking a rematch.
- Vale leads their singles series **1–0**.

This is intended as a long-term Chekhov's gun. The rematch should be held back until it feels genuinely important.

---

### SLC Challenge Championship
**Velociraptor (c) vs Behemoth vs Trayvon Steel vs Dee Woodland**

**Fatal Four-Way Elimination Match**

- Quo Zabay uses his influence to get all three members of Pavement Cartel into Velociraptor's title defence.
- Under SLC rules, all Fatal Four-Way Championship matches use elimination rules.
- Behemoth's proposed strategy is for Pavement Cartel to work together to eliminate Velociraptor first, after which the remaining Cartel members can fight among themselves for the championship.
- Trayvon Steel and Dee Woodland believe the situation is sneaky and underhanded but agree to wrestle the match because it has already been booked.
- Before the match, an angry Velociraptor tells Kiana Martins that by the time he is finished with them they will be **"The Corpse Cartel."**
- Lemonbeck is entertained by Behemoth's increasingly ruthless ambition.
- Limebeck considers the title defence grossly unfair to Velociraptor.
- **Current plan: Behemoth wins the SLC Challenge Championship.**
- The result begins increasing tension between Behemoth and the other members of Pavement Cartel.
- Behemoth's relationship with Zabay continues after the title win.

---

### Hardcore Match
**Demolition Jones vs Douglas Wright**

- Beginning of SLC giving hardcore wrestling a more prominent place within its overall mix of styles.
- Demolition Jones is established as SLC's primary hardcore specialist.
- Douglas Wright begins moving toward a heel role.
- The rivalry is intended to continue beyond this match.
- **Current plan: Demolition Jones wins.**

---

### SLC Tag Team Championship
**Champagne Syndicate (c) vs The Vertex**

- Follows the 20-minute time-limit draw at Trial By Combat.
- Justin Gabriel and Heath Slater finally get another opportunity against the champions.
- **Current plan: The Vertex win the SLC Tag Team Championship.**
- Champagne Syndicate subsequently move toward joining Industry Standard.
- Vertex are intended to receive a genuine championship reign rather than immediately dropping the belts back.

---

### Desmond Boyce vs Mark Henry

- Mark Henry is now working as a babyface.
- First match in a two-match Boyce/Henry programme.
- **Current plan: Desmond Boyce wins.**
- Boyce uses the victory to argue that Henry's best years are behind him.
- Their second match will carry much larger stakes.

# Long-Term Story Threads

## Dante Navarro vs Simon Vale
- First major match planned for PPV 1.
- Vale wins clean after an approximately 35-minute classic.
- No immediate rematch.
- Intended as a long-term defining SLC rivalry in the tradition of recurring generational pairings.
- Vale initially leads the series 1–0.

## Industry Standard
Current core:
- Matt Riddle
- Simon Vale
- Yoon Tae-Seok
- Kevin Nash

Planned addition:
- Champagne Syndicate

Champagne Syndicate may join while or after losing the Tag Team Championship, with the current direction leading to them eventually regaining the titles with assistance from Riddle.

The group should remain extremely powerful without holding every SLC championship simultaneously.

## Pavement Cartel / Behemoth / Quo Zabay
- Behemoth begins doing business with Quo Zabay.
- Zabay secures the four-way Challenge Championship opportunity.
- Trayvon Steel and Dee Woodland dislike the underhanded methods but initially remain loyal to Behemoth.
- Behemoth becomes Challenge Champion.
- Behemoth increasingly uses Zabay to secure opportunities for both himself and Pavement Cartel.
- Trayvon and Dee become progressively more frustrated with how those opportunities are obtained.
- The group deteriorates gradually rather than breaking up immediately.
- Behemoth eventually separates from Pavement Cartel.
- Pavement Cartel continues as **Trayvon Steel & Dee Woodland**.

## Quo Zabay
- The Delegation concept is being phased out/reconsidered.
- Zabay moves toward a role as an independent political operator, fixer and negotiator.
- Wrestlers can do business with him without becoming permanent members of a stable.
- His influence outside the ring becomes more important than protecting his personal win-loss record.

## SLC Hardcore Wrestling
Primary figures:
- Demolition Jones
- Douglas Wright
- Buck Harwood

Additional wrestlers may occasionally work the style, including established roster members and future Dojo call-ups.

The hardcore scene is intended to be a recognised **style within SLC**, not a separate promotion or mandatory division.

Demolition Jones remains its defining specialist.

## Women's Wrestling
- Women's wrestling becomes a permanent part of SLC beginning with PPV 1.
- Initial roster, championship format and first major programmes remain TBD.
- The division should contain a mix of wrestling styles and character types.
- Women's wrestling should be integrated into SLC's normal sports-based presentation rather than treated as a separate attraction.

## Mark Henry / Desmond Boyce
- PPV 1: Boyce vs Henry I.
- Boyce wins.
- PPV 2: Boyce vs Henry II with Henry's SLC career on the line.
- Intended to significantly elevate Boyce regardless of Henry's longer-term status.

## The Vertex / Champagne Syndicate
- Trial By Combat: 20-minute draw.
- PPV 1: Vertex defeat Champagne Syndicate for the Tag Team Championship.
- Vertex receive approximately two successful title defences.
- One possible defence is against Pavement Cartel during the Behemoth/Zabay tension.
- Champagne Syndicate join Industry Standard.
- PPV 4: Champagne Syndicate regain the championships with assistance from Matt Riddle.
- Champion's Way: Champagne defend against Vertex and Pavement Cartel.
